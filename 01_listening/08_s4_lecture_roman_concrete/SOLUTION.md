## Questions 31–40 · Completion (sentence / note / table / form / summary)

> **Strategy:** Look at the words just before and after each gap: they are usually paraphrased, not repeated exactly. E.g. note 'weak at first' ← lecturer 'much weaker … when it's first made'.

**31. Naples** — 'the town of Pozzuoli, near Naples'.

**32. brick, bricks** — 'chunks of rock or broken brick'.

**33. unreinforced** — 'the largest unreinforced concrete dome in the world'. Hyphen-free single word.

**34. pumice** — 'they used lighter volcanic rock, called pumice'.

**35. crystals, minerals** — 'form new minerals, including rare crystals that grow in the gaps and reinforce the material'.

**36. hot** — 'mixing the lime while it was still hot'.

**37. repair, heal** — 'The concrete, in effect, repairs itself.' Note the base form is needed after 'can'.

**38. strength** — 'it takes a long time to reach full strength'.

**39. temperatures, temperature** — 'produced at significantly lower temperatures'.

**40. walls** — 'for use in sea walls'.
