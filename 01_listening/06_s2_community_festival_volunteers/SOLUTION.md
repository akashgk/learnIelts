## Questions 11–15 · Multiple choice (one answer)

**11. B** — 'partly because the weather forecast is good, but mainly because we've moved to the larger site'.
  
  *Trap:* 'partly … but mainly' — the question asks for the MAIN reason.

**12. A** — 'this time the theme is sustainability' — recycling, local food, energy saving.

**13. C** — 'Please come to the volunteer tent, not the main entrance'.

**14. A** — 'you'll be given a T-shirt … We'll also give you a voucher for a free lunch'.

**15. B** — 'Contact the nearest steward'.

## Questions 16–20 · Matching (headings, information, features, endings)

> **Strategy:** Two options are extra. Each team is described in order; listen for the specific responsibility, not general words that appear in several descriptions.

**16. C** — Counting visitors 'because we need accurate figures for our funding applications'.

**17. G** — 'last year a lot of recycling was rejected because it was contaminated'.

**18. D** — 'You won't be responsible for supervising children on your own'.

**19. E** — 'please wear sensible shoes'.
  
  *Trap:* B — 'You don't need technical skills'.

**20. A** — 'You'll be handling money … collect a cash box'.
