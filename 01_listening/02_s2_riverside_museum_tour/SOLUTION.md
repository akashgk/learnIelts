## Questions 11–15 · Multiple choice (one answer)

> **Strategy:** In Section 2 MCQs, all three options are usually mentioned. Underline the key idea in the question stem and listen for the speaker's FINAL position on it.

**11. B** — 'originally a warehouse, built in 1868 to store cotton'.

**12. A** — 'Photography is allowed everywhere except in the textile gallery'.
  
  *Trap:* Special exhibitions are mentioned — but in connection with a charge, not photography.

**13. C** — 'goes towards the restoration of the building' — restoration = repairing.

**14. B** — 'what we're most short of … are people to help children with our hands-on activities at weekends'.
  
  *Trap:* A — 'People often assume we need historians, but…'

**15. B** — 'we're setting up for an evening event'.
  
  *Trap:* C — refurbishment is mentioned later, for a room, not the closing time.

## Questions 16–20 · Matching (headings, information, features, endings)

> **Strategy:** Locate the entrance and 'you are here' first. Follow the speaker's directions with your pencil: 'on your right', 'opposite', 'in the north-east corner', 'between'.

**16. H** — 'immediately on your right, in the south-east corner' — entering from the south, right = east.

**17. F** — 'directly opposite the café, on the other side of the entrance — the room on your left' = south-west.

**18. B** — 'three rooms along the north wall. The one in the middle'.

**19. C** — 'on the right, in the north-east corner'.

**20. E** — 'on the east side of the main hall, between the café and the discovery room'.
  
  *Trap:* D (west side) is the room closed for refurbishment.
