## Questions 1–10 · Completion (sentence / note / table / form / summary)

> **Strategy:** Several numbers are heard close together (40, 60, 120; £45, £100). Decide which number answers the specific note.

**1. 120, a hundred and twenty, one hundred and twenty** — 'The Main Hall takes up to a hundred and twenty.'
  
  *Trap:* 40 is the Garden Room; 60 is the number of guests.

**2. 20, 20th, twentieth** — Saturday 19th is booked; Sunday the twentieth is free.

**3. 5, five, 5.00, 5:00** — 'You can have access from five o'clock'.

**4. 100, one hundred** — 'a refundable deposit of one hundred pounds'.
  
  *Trap:* £45 is the hourly rate.

**5. glasses** — 'we don't provide plates or glasses'.

**6. 10.45, 10:45, ten forty-five** — 'all music has to finish by ten forty-five'.
  
  *Trap:* 11 is when the party ends.

**7. 30, thirty** — 'thirty spaces'.

**8. Pritchard** — Spelled P-R-I-T-C-H-A-R-D.

**9. 7, seven** — 'within seven days'.

**10. 07854316207, 07854 316 207** — '07854 316 207'.
