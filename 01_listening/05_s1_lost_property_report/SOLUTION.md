## Questions 1–10 · Completion (sentence / note / table / form / summary)

> **Strategy:** Dates and self-corrections are common in Section 1. Write the date in any clear form (13th, 13 or thirteenth).

**1. Ashworth** — Spelled A-S-H-W-O-R-T-H.

**2. 13th, 13, thirteenth, the 13th** — She first says 'Thursday the fourteenth' then corrects: 'It was Wednesday, the thirteenth'.
  
  *Trap:* 14th is today's date.

**3. luggage rack** — 'I put the bag on the luggage rack above my seat'.

**4. canvas** — 'made of canvas'. Leather is only the straps.
  
  *Trap:* 'Leather' is heard, but it describes the straps, not the bag.

**5. penguin** — 'it's a picture of a penguin'.

**6. umbrella, an umbrella** — 'And an umbrella.'
  
  *Trap:* Cards are mentioned — but she keeps them separately.

**7. sticker** — 'a sticker on the bottom with my email address'.

**8. 01614960387, 0161 496 0387** — Said once — numbers in groups help you write quickly.

**9. LP 2296, LP2296** — 'Your reference number is LP 2296', repeated by the woman.

**10. identification, ID, passport, driving licence** — 'You'll need to bring some form of identification — a passport or a driving licence.' Any of these fits the gap.
