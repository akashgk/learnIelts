## Questions 1–10 · Completion (sentence / note / table / form / summary)

> **Strategy:** Section 1 tests spelling and numbers. Before the audio starts, predict each gap: a name? a number? a day? Listen for corrections ('no, sorry…') — the first thing you hear is often a distractor.

**1. Kowalski** — Spelled out letter by letter: K-O-W-A-L-S-K-I. Spelling must be exact.

**2. Thornbury** — Spelled out: T-H-O-R-N-B-U-R-Y.

**3. 07723580914, 07723 580 914** — The number is said and then repeated by the receptionist — use the repetition to check.

**4. Standard** — Off-Peak is rejected (he works in the day) and Premium is rejected (no sauna or classes).
  
  *Trap:* All three types are mentioned; wait for his final decision.

**5. 15, fifteen** — 'normally twenty-five pounds, but this month we've reduced it to fifteen'.
  
  *Trap:* 25 is the normal fee — a classic distractor.

**6. friend** — He first says 'a poster at the library' then corrects himself: 'It was a friend from work'.
  
  *Trap:* Self-correction — 'poster' is wrong.

**7. Tuesday, Tuesdays** — 'lanes are reserved for the swimming club on Tuesday evenings'. Days need a capital letter in the real test.

**8. registration** — 'register your car's registration number at reception'.

**9. induction** — 'all new members have to attend an induction session'.

**10. photo, photograph** — 'please bring a photo for your membership card'.
