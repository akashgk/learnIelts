## Questions 11–15 · Multiple choice (one answer)

**11. B** — 'the land was already owned by the city, which saved a great deal of money'.
  
  *Trap:* C is a benefit mentioned afterwards, not the reason for choosing it.

**12. C** — 'later than any other public library in the region'.

**13. A** — 'bring proof of your address'. A photo is NOT needed — staff take one.

**14. B** — 'Most impressive … is the roof, which collects rainwater'.

**15. B** — 'free but must be booked online'.

## Questions 16–20 · Matching (headings, information, features, endings)

**16. E** — Newspapers and magazines 'from around the world' = international publications.

**17. B** — Homework clubs after school for older children.

**18. F** — 'library of things' — tools, sewing machines, camping equipment.

**19. A** — 'strictly silent — no phone calls'.

**20. C** — 'you'll need to make an appointment in advance'.
