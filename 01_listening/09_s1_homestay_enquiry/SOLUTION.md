## Questions 1–10 · Completion (sentence / note / table / form / summary)

> **Strategy:** Listen for changes of mind ('I was going to say… but actually…'). Numbers often appear twice — the second one is usually the answer.

**1. Ferretti** — Spelled F-E-R-R-E-T-T-I.

**2. 23, twenty-three** — 'I'm twenty-three.'

**3. 6, 6th, sixth** — He first mentions the eighth, then changes to 'the sixth of September'.
  
  *Trap:* 8th is the course start date.

**4. 10, ten** — Course is twelve weeks but he wants 'ten weeks with the family'.
  
  *Trap:* 12 is the course length.

**5. fish** — 'I don't eat meat, but I do eat fish.'

**6. cats** — 'I'm allergic to cats.' Dogs are fine.

**7. 165** — 'one hundred and sixty-five pounds a week'.

**8. walking** — 'within walking distance of the school'.

**9. 5, five** — 'within five working days'.

**10. coach** — 'meet you at the coach station'.
