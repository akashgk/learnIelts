## Questions 21–24 · Multiple choice (one answer)

**21. B** — Omar: 'infrastructure on its own isn't enough — the cities that succeeded also made driving less convenient'; Lena agrees.
  
  *Trap:* A was Omar's FIRST idea, which he abandons ('at first I thought…').

**22. C** — Lena suggests 'a photo of the same street before and after'; Omar: 'Good idea'.

**23. B** — 'Let's just do the one at the end' — asking 'what would make them cycle more'.

**24. B** — 20 minutes minus 5 for questions = 15, divided by two.

## Questions 25–30 · Matching (headings, information, features, endings)

**25. A** — Superhighways 'target commuters who live too far away to cycle on ordinary roads'.

**26. E** — 'it costs very little, because it uses roads that already exist'.

**27. D** — 'built a whole network of separated lanes in just a few years'.

**28. B** — 'very controversial … huge protests from drivers and some businesses'.

**29. C** — 'combining cycling with public transport … cycle to the station and take the train'.

**30. G** — 'It's cheap and it's easy to reverse if it doesn't work.' Both E and G are true of the trial, but E is already used for Bogotá, and 'easy to reverse' = 'can easily be undone'.
  
  *Trap:* Each option is used once in this task.
