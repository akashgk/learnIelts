# IELTS Academic: test format

IELTS Academic tests **Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking**. Total time is about **2 hours 45 minutes**. Listening, Reading and Writing are taken on the same day with no breaks. Speaking can be on the same day or up to a week before or after.

| Section | Time | Parts | Questions | What's tested |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Listening** | ~30 min (+ checking time) | 4 recordings | 40 | Understanding main ideas, specific facts, opinions, how ideas develop |
| **Reading** | 60 min | 3 passages (~2,150–2,750 words total) | 40 | Skimming, scanning, detail, inference, the writer's views |
| **Writing** | 60 min | Task 1 (≥150 words) + Task 2 (≥250 words) | 2 | Describing visual data; arguing a position |
| **Speaking** | 11–14 min | Part 1, Part 2, Part 3 | — | Fluency, vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation |

## Listening (40 questions)
| Part | Situation | Example in this repo |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Conversation, everyday social context (booking, enquiry) | `01_listening/01_s1_…`, `05_s1_…` |
| 2 | Monologue, everyday context (tour, briefing, radio talk) | `02_s2_…`, `06_s2_…` |
| 3 | Conversation in an education setting (students + tutor) | `03_s3_…`, `07_s3_…` |
| 4 | Academic lecture (monologue, no break) | `04_s4_…`, `08_s4_…` |

- You hear each recording **once only**.
- Spelling counts. Answers that go over the word limit are marked wrong.
- On the computer-delivered test you type answers directly and get **2 minutes** at the end to check. On paper you had 10 minutes to transfer answers.

## Reading (40 questions)
- Three long texts from books, journals, magazines and newspapers, written for a non-specialist audience.
- Passages get harder from 1 to 3. **Spend about 20 minutes on each.**
- There is **no extra transfer time**. Write answers as you go.

## Writing
| | Task 1 | Task 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Time (suggested) | 20 min | 40 min |
| Minimum words | 150 | 250 |
| Weight | ⅓ of Writing score | ⅔ of Writing score |
| Task | Describe a graph, table, chart, diagram, process or map | Essay responding to a point of view, argument or problem |

## Speaking (face-to-face with an examiner, recorded)
| Part | Time | Format |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4–5 min | Questions about familiar topics (home, work, hobbies) |
| 2 | 3–4 min | Cue card: 1 minute to prepare, then speak for up to 2 minutes |
| 3 | 4–5 min | Abstract discussion linked to the Part 2 topic |

## Delivery changes (2026)
- IELTS is moving to **computer-delivered tests** in most markets. The last paper-based test date in most markets was **27 June 2026**, and rollout varies by country.
- In some markets you can choose **Writing on Paper** (handwrite the Writing section while the rest is on computer).
- The **content, question types and scoring are unchanged**.
- **IELTS One Skill Retake** is available in some locations: you can retake one section without retaking the whole test.

> Always confirm current arrangements with your test centre (British Council, IDP or Cambridge).
