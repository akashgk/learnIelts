# Test day checklist

## The day before
- [ ] Check your test centre address, start time and travel time.
- [ ] Prepare the **same ID** (passport / national ID) you used to register.
- [ ] Sleep properly. Don't learn new material tonight.

## On the day
- [ ] Arrive early. Late candidates may not be allowed in.
- [ ] Only ID and clear water bottle (rules vary). Phones and watches go in storage.
- [ ] On computer: check the headphone volume before Listening starts.

## Listening
- Use every pause to read ahead.
- Never leave a blank. There's no penalty for wrong answers.
- Check spelling and plurals in the final checking time.

## Reading
- **20 minutes per passage**, strictly. Move on if you're stuck.
- Do the question types you're fastest at first within each passage.
- Fill every gap before time runs out.

## Writing
- **Task 2 first** is an option many candidates prefer, because it's worth twice as much.
- Spend 5 minutes planning Task 2. Keep 3 minutes at the end to check articles, plurals and subject–verb agreement.
- Don't count every word. Know roughly how many lines your handwriting or typing makes 250 words.

## Speaking
- It's a conversation, not an interrogation. Smile, and extend your answers.
- If you don't understand, ask: *"Could you repeat the question, please?"* (No penalty in Parts 1 and 3.)
- Need thinking time? Use a natural filler: *"That's an interesting question — I haven't really thought about it before, but…"*
- In Part 2, keep talking until the examiner stops you.
