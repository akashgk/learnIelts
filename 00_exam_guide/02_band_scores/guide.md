# Band scores: how IELTS is marked

Each section gets a band from **0 to 9** in half-band steps. The **overall band** is the average of the four, rounded to the nearest half band:
- an average ending in **.25** rounds **up** to .5 (6.25 → 6.5)
- an average ending in **.75** rounds **up** to the next whole band (6.75 → 7.0)
- an average ending in .1 or .6 rounds down (6.1 → 6.0, 6.6 → 6.5)

**Example:** L 7.5 + R 7.0 + W 6.0 + S 6.5 = 27.0 ÷ 4 = 6.75 → **Overall 7.0**

## Listening and Reading: raw score → band (approximate)

The exact conversion changes slightly between test versions. These are the commonly published approximate values for **Academic** Reading and Listening.

| Raw /40 | Listening band | Academic Reading band |

| 39–40 | 9.0 | 9.0 |
| 37–38 | 8.5 | 8.5 |
| 35–36 | 8.0 | 8.0 |
| 33–34 | 7.5 | 7.5 |
| 32 | 7.5 | 7.0 |
| 30–31 | 7.0 | 7.0 |
| 27–29 | 6.5 | 6.5 |
| 26 | 6.5 | 6.0 |

> **Rule of thumb:** Band 7 ≈ 30/40 in both Listening and Reading.

## Writing: four criteria, equal weight

| Criterion | Band 9 looks like | Band 6 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Task Achievement** (T1) / **Task Response** (T2) | Fully addresses every part of the task. Clear overview (T1). Fully developed position (T2). | Addresses the task, but some parts are covered better than others. The overview may be unclear. Ideas are not always well supported. |
| **Coherence & Cohesion** | Cohesion "attracts no attention". Paragraphing is handled skilfully. | Organisation is clear overall, but linkers may be mechanical, overused or faulty. |
| **Lexical Resource** | Wide, natural, sophisticated vocabulary. Errors are rare "slips". | Adequate range, some attempt at less common words. Some errors that don't block meaning. |
| **Grammatical Range & Accuracy** | Wide range of structures, fully flexible and accurate. | Mix of simple and complex sentences. Some errors, but they rarely reduce communication. |

**Task 2 counts twice as much as Task 1.** Writing band ≈ (T1 + 2 × T2) ÷ 3, rounded.

### Penalties to avoid
- **Under length** (<150 / <250 words): lowers Task Achievement/Response.
- **Off-topic or memorised** text: may not be counted.
- **Copying the question**: copied words aren't counted toward length.
- **No overview** (Task 1): usually caps Task Achievement at Band 5.
- **No clear position** (Task 2, when one is asked for): limits Task Response.

## Speaking: four criteria, equal weight

| Criterion | What the examiner listens for |
|---|---|
| **Fluency & Coherence** | Speaking at length without long pauses. Logical sequencing. Natural discourse markers. |
| **Lexical Resource** | Range, precision, idiomatic language, ability to paraphrase. |
| **Grammatical Range & Accuracy** | Complex structures (conditionals, relative clauses, passives) used accurately. |
| **Pronunciation** | Clear individual sounds, word stress, intonation, rhythm. Accent is **not** penalised. |
