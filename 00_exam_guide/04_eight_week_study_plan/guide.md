# 8-week study plan (about 1.5–2 hours a day)

The plan uses only the material in this repo plus free official tests (see `resources/README.md`). Item IDs refer to folder names, e.g. `R01` = `02_reading/01_…`.

**Legend:** R = Reading · L = Listening · W1/W2 = Writing Task 1/2 · S1/S23 = Speaking Part 1 / Parts 2–3 · V = Vocabulary · G = Grammar

## How to work through a practice item
1. **Attempt** under time (Reading 20 min/passage; Listening: no pausing).
2. **Mark** with `SOLUTION.md` (or the app built on `index.json`).
3. **Analyse every error:** was it vocabulary, a paraphrase you missed, a trap, the word limit, or spelling? Keep an error log.
4. **Learn** the language: add 5 new words or phrases from the item to your vocabulary notebook.

## Writing routine
1. Write your own answer within the time limit **before** reading the model.
2. Compare it with the model against the four criteria. Underline 3 phrases to reuse.
3. Rewrite your weakest paragraph.

## Speaking routine
1. Answer the questions out loud and **record yourself**.
2. Read the model answer and note useful phrases, then answer again using 2–3 of them, adapted to your own life.
3. For Part 2, practise with a timer: 1 minute of notes, 2 minutes of speaking.
