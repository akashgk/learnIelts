# Writing Task 1 — Mixed charts (line graph + bar chart)

*You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.*

**The line graph shows the number of international tourists visiting one country between 2010 and 2022. The bar chart shows the main purpose of their visits in 2022.**

**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

*Write at least 150 words.*

**Line graph — international arrivals (millions)**

| 2010 | 2012 | 2014 | 2016 | 2018 | 2020 | 2022 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8.0 | 9.5 | 11.2 | 13.0 | 15.5 | 3.1 | 11.8 |

**Bar chart — purpose of visit, 2022 (%)**

| Holiday | Visiting friends / family | Business | Study |
|---|---|---|---|
| 58 | 22 | 14 | 6 |
