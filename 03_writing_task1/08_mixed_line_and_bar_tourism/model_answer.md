# Model answer (Band 8–9)

The line graph illustrates how many overseas tourists visited a particular country from 2010 to 2022, while the bar chart breaks down the reasons for their visits in the final year.

Overall, tourist numbers grew steadily for most of the period before collapsing in 2020 and then partially recovering. In 2022, the great majority of visitors came for leisure purposes, with holidays being by far the most common reason.

In 2010, the country received 8 million international visitors. This figure rose consistently over the next eight years, almost doubling to a peak of 15.5 million in 2018. However, arrivals then plummeted to just 3.1 million in 2020, roughly a fifth of the peak level. By 2022, numbers had rebounded to 11.8 million, although this was still well below the 2018 high.

Turning to the purpose of visits in 2022, 58% of tourists came on holiday, and a further 22% travelled to see friends or relatives. Business trips accounted for 14% of visits, while study was the least common reason, at only 6%.

## Examiner notes

- With two different charts, write **one body paragraph per chart** and make the overview cover **both**.
- *"collapsing in 2020"* — the data doesn't say why. A Band 9 answer does **not** mention a pandemic, because that is outside knowledge.
- **Peak / trough language:** *reached a peak of, peaked at, hit a low of, rebounded, recovered, still well below*.
- **Linking the charts:** *"…the reasons for their visits in the final year"* tells the reader how the two charts connect.
