# Writing Task 1 — Bar chart (two years, age groups)

*You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.*

**The bar chart below shows the average number of hours per day that people in different age groups spent online in one country in 2012 and 2022.**

**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

*Write at least 150 words.*

| Age group | 2012 (hours/day) | 2022 (hours/day) |
|---|---|---|
| 16–24 | 3.5 | 6.2 |
| 25–34 | 3.0 | 5.4 |
| 35–54 | 2.1 | 4.1 |
| 55–64 | 1.2 | 3.0 |
| 65+ | 0.5 | 2.2 |
