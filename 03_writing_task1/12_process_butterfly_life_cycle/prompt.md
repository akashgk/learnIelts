# Writing Task 1 — Process

*You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.*

**The diagram below shows the life cycle of a butterfly.**

**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

*Write at least 150 words.*

```
  [1] Adult female lays eggs on a leaf (100–300 eggs)
        ↓  3–7 days
  [2] Egg hatches → caterpillar (larva) eats leaves, grows, sheds skin 4–5 times
        ↓  2–3 weeks
  [3] Caterpillar attaches to stem → forms chrysalis (pupa)
        ↓  10–14 days (body transforms inside)
  [4] Adult butterfly emerges, dries wings for a few hours
        ↓  mates (adult lives 2–4 weeks)
  └──────────────► back to [1]
```
