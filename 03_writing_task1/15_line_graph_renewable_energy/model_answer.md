# Model answer (Band 8–9)

The line graph shows the proportion of electricity produced from renewable sources in four countries over a twenty-five-year period from 2000.

Overall, the share of renewable electricity rose in all four countries. Country A generated the highest proportion throughout, but the most rapid growth occurred in the other three, particularly from 2010 onwards, which significantly narrowed the gap.

In 2000, Country A already obtained more than half of its electricity (55%) from renewable sources, far ahead of Country B (15%), Country C (8%) and Country D (just 2%). Over the next decade, Country A's figure increased only marginally, to 58%, while the other countries also saw modest gains.

After 2010, growth accelerated markedly in Countries B, C and D. Country B's share more than doubled to 52% by 2025, and Country C's rose more than threefold, from 12% to 43%. Although Country D remained the lowest throughout, it experienced the most dramatic proportional increase, reaching 28% in 2025 — fourteen times its 2000 level. Meanwhile, Country A continued its steady rise, finishing the period at 70%.

- **Proportional language:** *more than doubled, more than threefold, fourteen times its 2000 level*.
