# Model answer (Band 8–9)

The maps compare the current layout of a university campus with proposed changes to it.

Overall, the redevelopment will make the campus more residential and less car-oriented. The main changes involve the northern half of the site, where new buildings will be constructed, and the removal of vehicles from the surface of the campus. The central academic buildings and the woodland, by contrast, will largely remain as they are.

In the north of the campus, part of the sports field in the north-west will be used for an indoor sports centre, leaving a smaller outdoor field. The large car park in the north-east is set to be replaced by student accommodation, and parking will instead be provided in a new underground facility beneath the centre of the campus.

The library, lecture halls and science building will all stay in their existing positions, although the library is to be extended to the west and a new laboratory will be added to the science building. In the south, the main gate will be restricted to pedestrians and cyclists, and a café will be built near the bus stop. The woodland in the south-east will be preserved.

*(194 words)*

## Examiner notes

- Planned changes need **future forms**: *will be constructed, is set to be replaced, is to be extended, will be preserved*. Vary them.
- **Overview** = the overall character of the change (more residential, fewer cars) + what stays the same.
- Each feature that changes is mentioned once — accuracy beats detail.
