# Writing Task 1 — Table

*You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.*

**The table below gives information about public transport use, average commuting time and the cost of a monthly travel pass in five cities.**

**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

*Write at least 150 words.*

| City | Commuters using public transport (%) | Average one-way commute (minutes) | Monthly pass (US$) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tokyo | 68 | 48 | 95 |
| New York | 55 | 52 | 132 |
| London | 45 | 46 | 210 |
| Berlin | 40 | 38 | 60 |
| Sydney | 25 | 42 | 150 |
