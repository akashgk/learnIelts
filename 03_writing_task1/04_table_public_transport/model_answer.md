## Examiner notes

- Tables have **no visual trend**, so you must find the story yourself: highest/lowest in each column and any interesting relationship (or lack of one).
- Organise by **column** (usage → cost → time) rather than city by city, so you can compare.
- *"There appears to be little connection…"* is an acceptable comparison drawn from the data — it is not an outside opinion.
- Note the careful hedging: *appears to be*. Avoid claiming causes.

## Comparison structures

- *X was roughly three and a half times the price of Y.*
- *X and Y had similar figures of … and … respectively.*
- *ranging from … in A to … in B*
- *followed by … at …*
- *by far the most expensive*
