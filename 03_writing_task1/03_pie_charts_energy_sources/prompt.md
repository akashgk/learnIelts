# Writing Task 1 — Two pie charts

*You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.*

**The pie charts below show the sources of electricity generated in one country in 2000 and 2020.**

**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

*Write at least 150 words.*

| Source | 2000 (%) | 2020 (%) |
|---|---|---|
| Coal | 45 | 18 |
| Natural gas | 25 | 32 |
| Nuclear | 15 | 14 |
| Hydroelectric | 10 | 11 |
| Wind & solar | 2 | 22 |
| Oil | 3 | 3 |
