# Model answer (Band 8–9)

The two pie charts illustrate how the electricity produced in a particular country was generated in 2000 and 2020.

It is clear that the country's energy mix shifted significantly away from coal over the two decades. Whereas coal was by far the dominant source at the start of the period, by 2020 natural gas had become the largest contributor, and renewable sources such as wind and solar had grown from a negligible share to a major one.

In 2000, coal-fired power stations produced almost half of the nation's electricity (45%), followed by natural gas at a quarter. Wind and solar power were barely used, accounting for only 2% of the total. Twenty years later, the share generated from coal had fallen by more than half, to 18%, while gas rose to 32%. The most remarkable change was in wind and solar energy, which expanded more than tenfold to 22%, overtaking coal.

The remaining sources were relatively stable. Nuclear power contributed 15% in 2000 and a similar 14% in 2020, hydroelectric power edged up from 10% to 11%, and oil remained the smallest source, at just 3% in both years.

*(189 words)*

---

## Examiner notes

- With two pie charts, compare **the same slice across both years**, not one chart then the other.
- **Overview** names the two main stories: coal's decline and the rise of gas and renewables.
- Notice how few numbers are needed for the stable categories; group them in one sentence.
- Language for pies: *accounted for, made up, constituted, the share of, contributed, a negligible share, the dominant source, overtaking*.

## Band 6 vs Band 8 sentence

- **Band 6:** *In 2000 coal was 45% and in 2020 coal was 18%. Gas was 25% and it went up to 32%.*
- **Band 8:** *The share generated from coal had fallen by more than half, to 18%, while gas rose to 32%.*

The second version **combines** data, uses **precise verbs** and a **past perfect** to show the change over time.
