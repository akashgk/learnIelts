# Writing Task 1 — Mixed

*You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.*

**The pie chart shows how university students in one country spend their monthly budget. The table shows the average monthly budget in three types of accommodation.**

**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

*Write at least 150 words.*

**Pie chart — share of monthly spending (%)**

| Rent | Food | Transport | Course materials | Leisure | Phone & internet | Other |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 45 | 20 | 8 | 5 | 12 | 4 | 6 |

**Table — average monthly budget by accommodation (US$)**

| Accommodation | Total budget | Of which rent |
|---|---|---|
| University halls | 1,100 | 520 |
| Shared private flat | 1,250 | 600 |
| Living with parents | 600 | 50 |
