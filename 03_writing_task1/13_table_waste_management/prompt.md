# Writing Task 1 — Table

*You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.*

**The table below shows how household waste was managed in four countries in 2022.**

**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

*Write at least 150 words.*

| Country | Recycled (%) | Composted (%) | Incinerated (%) | Landfill (%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Country A | 48 | 18 | 32 | 2 |
| Country B | 27 | 9 | 14 | 50 |
| Country C | 35 | 15 | 45 | 5 |
| Country D | 12 | 3 | 5 | 80 |
