# Model answer (Band 8–9)

The diagram illustrates the process by which used plastic bottles are recycled and turned into new products.

Overall, the process consists of eight stages, beginning with the disposal of bottles by consumers and ending with the manufacture of a range of new goods. The main transformation takes place at the recycling plant, where the bottles are broken down and converted into small pellets.

First, empty bottles are placed in recycling bins, from which they are collected by truck and transported to a recycling centre. On arrival, they are sorted according to the type of plastic and their colour. The sorted bottles are then crushed and compressed into bales, before being shredded into small flakes.

At the next stage, these flakes are washed in order to remove any remaining labels and glue. Once they have been cleaned and dried, they are melted down and formed into pellets. Finally, the pellets are sent to factories, where they are used to produce a variety of items, including new bottles, fibres for clothing, carpets and packaging materials.

## Examiner notes

- **Passive voice** is essential for man-made processes: *are collected, are sorted, are melted down*. The agent (workers, machines) is unimportant.
- **Sequencers** guide the reader: *First, then, before being, At the next stage, Once…, Finally*. Vary them — don't write "then" eight times.
- **Overview**: number of stages + start and end point + the key transformation.
- Every stage in the diagram is mentioned — missing a stage lowers Task Achievement.

## Sequencing toolkit

| Start | Middle | End |
|---|---|---|
| First / Firstly / To begin with / The process begins when… | Next / Then / After that / Subsequently / Following this / Once X has been…, / Before being… | Finally / In the final stage / Ultimately / The process ends with… |

**Passive tip:** *"After being shredded, the flakes are washed"* — a participle phrase avoids repeating the subject.
