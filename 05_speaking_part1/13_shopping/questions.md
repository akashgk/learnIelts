# Speaking Part 1 — Shopping

*The examiner asks 4–5 questions on a familiar topic. Answer each in 2–3 sentences (about 15–25 seconds).*

1. Do you enjoy shopping?
2. Do you prefer shopping online or in shops?
3. What was the last thing you bought?
4. Do you ever buy things you don't really need?
5. Has the way people shop changed in your country?
