# Model answers — Shopping

**1. Do you enjoy shopping?**

> It depends on what I'm buying. I quite enjoy browsing in bookshops, but shopping for clothes feels like a chore, so I usually try to get it over with as quickly as possible.

**2. Do you prefer shopping online or in shops?**

> For everyday things like household items, I definitely prefer online shopping because it saves time. But for clothes and shoes I'd rather go to a shop, so I can try things on first.

**3. What was the last thing you bought?**

> A pair of running shoes, actually. My old ones had completely worn out, and I'm training for a ten-kilometre race next month.

**4. Do you ever buy things you don't really need?**

> I'm afraid so — especially when there's a sale. I've got a drawer full of gadgets I used once and then forgot about.

**5. Has the way people shop changed in your country?**

> Enormously. Ten years ago most people went to local markets, but now delivery apps are everywhere, and you can get groceries delivered within twenty minutes in big cities.

---

## Useful phrases from these answers

- *It depends on what …*
- *get it over with*
- *I'd rather …, so I can …*
- *worn out*
- *I'm afraid so*

## How to answer Part 1 well
- **Length:** 2–3 sentences. Answer directly, then add a reason, example or detail.
- **Formula:** *Answer → Why / Example → (Extra detail)*
- **Don't** memorise — examiners notice scripted answers. Use these models to collect *language*, then answer about your own life.
- Change the details (city, job, food) to match your life; keep the structures.
