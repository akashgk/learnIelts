# Model answers — Holidays

**1. Where did you go on your last holiday?**

> I went to Goa with my cousins last December. We spent most of the time on the beach and trying the local seafood.

**2. Do you prefer relaxing or active holidays?**

> A mix of both, ideally. I like to relax for a few days, but I get restless if I don't do anything, so I usually plan a hike or a city tour as well.

**3. Do you like to plan holidays in advance?**

> I plan the main things, like flights and accommodation, but I prefer to leave the daily activities flexible so I can be spontaneous.

**4. Who do you usually go on holiday with?**

> Usually with friends now. When I was younger it was always with my family, and we'd visit relatives in other states.

**5. What kind of holiday would you like to have in the future?**

> I'd love to go on a long train journey across Europe, stopping in different cities along the way.

---

## Useful phrases from these answers

- *I get restless*
- *leave it flexible*
- *be spontaneous*
- *along the way*

## How to answer Part 1 well
- **Length:** 2–3 sentences. Answer directly, then add a reason, example or detail.
- **Formula:** *Answer → Why / Example → (Extra detail)*
- **Don't** memorise — examiners notice scripted answers. Use these models to collect *language*, then answer about your own life.
- Change the details (city, job, food) to match your life; keep the structures.
