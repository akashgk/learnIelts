# Model answers — Hometown

**1. Where is your hometown?**

> I'm from Kochi, a port city on the south-west coast of India, in the state of Kerala. It's surrounded by backwaters, so there's water almost everywhere you look.

**2. What do you like about your hometown?**

> I love the relaxed pace of life compared with bigger cities, and the food is fantastic — especially the seafood, which is incredibly fresh because it comes straight from the harbour.

**3. Has your hometown changed much in recent years?**

> Yes, quite dramatically. A metro line opened a few years ago, and there are far more shopping malls and apartment blocks than when I was a child. It's more convenient now, but some of the old character has been lost.

**4. Is it a good place for young people?**

> It's getting better. There are more jobs in IT and tourism than there used to be, although many young people still move to bigger cities like Bangalore for better career opportunities.

**5. Would you like to live there in the future?**

> Probably, yes — maybe not right now, while I'm building my career, but I can definitely see myself going back to settle down there later in life.

---

## Useful phrases from these answers

- *I'm from …, a … on the … coast*
- *quite dramatically*
- *some of the old character has been lost*
- *I can see myself …*

## How to answer Part 1 well
- **Length:** 2–3 sentences. Answer directly, then add a reason, example or detail.
- **Formula:** *Answer → Why / Example → (Extra detail)*
- **Don't** memorise — examiners notice scripted answers. Use these models to collect *language*, then answer about your own life.
- Change the details (city, job, food) to match your life; keep the structures.
