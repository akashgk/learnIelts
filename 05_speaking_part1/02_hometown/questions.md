# Speaking Part 1 — Hometown

*The examiner asks 4–5 questions on a familiar topic. Answer each in 2–3 sentences (about 15–25 seconds).*

1. Where is your hometown?
2. What do you like about your hometown?
3. Has your hometown changed much in recent years?
4. Is it a good place for young people?
5. Would you like to live there in the future?
