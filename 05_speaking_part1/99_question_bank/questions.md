# Speaking Part 1 — Question bank

Extra Part 1 topics as they appear in recent tests. Answer each question aloud in **2–3 sentences** (answer → reason/example → extra detail). Record yourself and check against the model answers in folders 01–20.

### Name
- Who gave you your name?
- Does your name have a special meaning?
- Would you like to change your name?

### Birthdays
- How do you usually celebrate your birthday?
- Do you remember a birthday from your childhood?
- Are birthdays important in your culture?

### Shopping
- Do you enjoy shopping?
- Do you prefer shopping online or in shops?
- What was the last thing you bought?
- Do you ever buy things you don't need?

### Clothes
- What kind of clothes do you usually wear?
- Do you wear different clothes at weekends?
- Have you ever worn a uniform?

### Neighbours
- Do you know your neighbours?
- How often do you see them?
- Are neighbours important to you?

### Art
- Do you like art?
- Have you ever been to an art gallery?
- Did you learn art at school?

### Photography
- Do you like taking photos?
- What do you usually photograph?
- Do you prefer printed photos or digital ones?

### Sport
- Do you play any sports?
- What sports are popular in your country?
- Did you do sport at school?
- Do you prefer watching or playing sport?

### Rain
- Do you like rainy days?
- What do you do when it rains?
- Does it rain a lot where you live?

### Social media
- Which social media do you use?
- How much time do you spend on it?
- Do you think you use it too much?

### Cooking
- Can you cook?
- Who usually cooks in your family?
- Would you like to learn to cook something new?

### Holidays
- Where did you go on your last holiday?
- Do you prefer relaxing or active holidays?
- Do you like to plan holidays in advance?

### Walking
- Do you walk much?
- Where do you like to go for a walk?
- Was walking more popular in the past?

### Gifts
- Do you like giving gifts?
- What was the best gift you've received?
- Is it difficult to choose gifts?

### Noise
- Is your home noisy?
- What noises do you dislike?
- Where do you go to find quiet?

### Mornings
- Are you a morning person?
- What do you usually do in the morning?
- Has your morning routine changed recently?

### Teachers
- Who was your favourite teacher?
- Would you like to be a teacher?
- What makes a good teacher?

### Libraries
- Do you often go to libraries?
- Did you use the library when you were a child?
- Will libraries still be important in the future?

### Plants
- Do you have any plants at home?
- Do you like gardening?
- Did you grow plants as a child?

### Public holidays
- What is the most important public holiday in your country?
- How do you spend public holidays?
- Should there be more public holidays?

### Emails and messages
- Do you write emails often?
- Do you prefer texting or calling?
- Do you reply to messages quickly?

### Hats and accessories
- Do you wear hats?
- Do you wear a watch?
- Are accessories important to you?

### Snacks
- Do you eat snacks?
- What snacks are popular in your country?
- Are snacks healthy?

### Bicycles
- Do you ride a bicycle?
- Is cycling popular in your city?
- Should cities build more cycle lanes?

### Museums
- Do you like visiting museums?
- Did you visit museums with your school?
- What kind of museum would you like to visit?

### Daydreaming
- Do you often daydream?
- When do you usually daydream?
- Is daydreaming useful?

### Colours
- What is your favourite colour?
- Are there colours you dislike?
- Do colours affect your mood?

### Patience
- Are you a patient person?
- When do you find it hard to be patient?
- Is patience important at work?

### Online learning
- Have you ever taken an online course?
- What do you like about learning online?
- Is online learning as good as classroom learning?

### Puzzles and games
- Do you like doing puzzles?
- What games did you play as a child?
- Do you play games on your phone?
