# Model answers — Free Time & Hobbies

**1. What do you like to do in your free time?**

> I'm really into photography at the moment. Most weekends I go out early in the morning to take pictures of the city before it gets busy.

**2. How long have you had this hobby?**

> For about three years now. I started during a holiday when I borrowed a friend's camera, and I was hooked almost immediately.

**3. Do you prefer spending free time alone or with others?**

> It depends on my mood. Photography is something I usually do on my own because it helps me relax, but I also love having dinner with friends at the weekend.

**4. Did you have the same hobbies when you were a child?**

> Not at all. As a child I was obsessed with football and spent every afternoon playing in the street with neighbours.

**5. Is there a new hobby you'd like to try?**

> I've always wanted to learn to play the guitar. I've bought one, actually, but I haven't found the time to take lessons yet!

---

## Useful phrases from these answers

- *I'm really into …*
- *I was hooked*
- *It depends on my mood*
- *Not at all.*
- *I've always wanted to …*

## How to answer Part 1 well
- **Length:** 2–3 sentences. Answer directly, then add a reason, example or detail.
- **Formula:** *Answer → Why / Example → (Extra detail)*
- **Don't** memorise — examiners notice scripted answers. Use these models to collect *language*, then answer about your own life.
- Change the details (city, job, food) to match your life; keep the structures.
