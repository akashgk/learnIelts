# Model answers — Mornings

**1. Are you a morning person?**

> Not naturally, but I've become one. I used to sleep late, but now I get up at six so I have some quiet time before work.

**2. What do you usually do in the morning?**

> I make coffee, do about twenty minutes of exercise and then check my messages while I have breakfast.

**3. Do you eat breakfast every day?**

> Almost always. If I skip it, I find it really hard to concentrate by mid-morning.

**4. Has your morning routine changed recently?**

> Yes, since I started working from home three days a week, my mornings are much less rushed because I don't have to commute.

**5. What do you think is the best way to start the day?**

> With something that isn't a screen, I think — a walk or some exercise. Checking emails first thing tends to make me stressed.

---

## Useful phrases from these answers

- *Not naturally, but …*
- *If I skip it, …*
- *much less rushed*
- *first thing*

## How to answer Part 1 well
- **Length:** 2–3 sentences. Answer directly, then add a reason, example or detail.
- **Formula:** *Answer → Why / Example → (Extra detail)*
- **Don't** memorise — examiners notice scripted answers. Use these models to collect *language*, then answer about your own life.
- Change the details (city, job, food) to match your life; keep the structures.
