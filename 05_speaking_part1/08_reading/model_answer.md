# Model answers — Reading

**1. Do you like reading?**

> Yes, very much — though I read less than I'd like to. I usually manage a novel every month or so, mainly on my commute.

**2. What kind of books do you read?**

> Mostly crime fiction and historical novels. I like stories that keep me guessing, and historical fiction is a painless way to learn about the past.

**3. Do you prefer paper books or e-books?**

> I prefer paper books, because I like the feel of them and it's easier to flick back to an earlier page. But e-books are more practical when I'm travelling.

**4. Did you read a lot as a child?**

> I did. My parents took me to the local library every Saturday, and I'd come home with a pile of books.

**5. Do you think children read less now than before?**

> On the whole, probably yes, because there's so much competition from screens. But there are still lots of young people who love reading, especially fantasy series.

---

## Useful phrases from these answers

- *I read less than I'd like to*
- *keep me guessing*
- *flick back*
- *On the whole, …*

## How to answer Part 1 well
- **Length:** 2–3 sentences. Answer directly, then add a reason, example or detail.
- **Formula:** *Answer → Why / Example → (Extra detail)*
- **Don't** memorise — examiners notice scripted answers. Use these models to collect *language*, then answer about your own life.
- Change the details (city, job, food) to match your life; keep the structures.
