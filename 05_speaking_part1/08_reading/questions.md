# Speaking Part 1 — Reading

*The examiner asks 4–5 questions on a familiar topic. Answer each in 2–3 sentences (about 15–25 seconds).*

1. Do you like reading?
2. What kind of books do you read?
3. Do you prefer paper books or e-books?
4. Did you read a lot as a child?
5. Do you think children read less now than before?
