# Model answers — Weather & Seasons

**1. What's the weather like where you live?**

> It's tropical, so it's hot and humid for most of the year, with heavy monsoon rains from June to September.

**2. What's your favourite season?**

> I'd go for winter — although 'winter' in my region just means it's slightly less hot! The air is drier and the evenings are really pleasant for walking.

**3. Does the weather affect your mood?**

> Definitely. When it's grey and raining for days on end, I tend to feel a bit lethargic, whereas a sunny morning puts me in a good mood straight away.

**4. Do you check the weather forecast?**

> Every morning, mainly to decide whether I need an umbrella. During the monsoon, that can make the difference between arriving at work dry or soaked.

**5. Has the weather changed since you were a child?**

> I think so. Summers seem to be getting hotter, and the rain seems less predictable — sometimes it's extremely heavy for a short time, which causes flooding.

---

## Useful phrases from these answers

- *I'd go for …*
- *for days on end*
- *lethargic*
- *puts me in a good mood*
- *less predictable*

## How to answer Part 1 well
- **Length:** 2–3 sentences. Answer directly, then add a reason, example or detail.
- **Formula:** *Answer → Why / Example → (Extra detail)*
- **Don't** memorise — examiners notice scripted answers. Use these models to collect *language*, then answer about your own life.
- Change the details (city, job, food) to match your life; keep the structures.
