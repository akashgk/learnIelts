# Speaking Part 1 — Sport & Exercise

*The examiner asks 4–5 questions on a familiar topic. Answer each in 2–3 sentences (about 15–25 seconds).*

1. Do you play any sports?
2. What sports are popular in your country?
3. Did you do sport at school?
4. Do you prefer watching or playing sport?
5. Do you think people do enough exercise nowadays?
