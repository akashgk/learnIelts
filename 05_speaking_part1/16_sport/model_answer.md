# Model answers — Sport & Exercise

**1. Do you play any sports?**

> I play badminton twice a week with some colleagues. It's a great way to switch off after work.

**2. What sports are popular in your country?**

> Cricket is by far the most popular — it's almost like a religion. Football has become more popular recently too, especially in my state.

**3. Did you do sport at school?**

> Yes, we had PE lessons twice a week, and I was in the school volleyball team for a couple of years.

**4. Do you prefer watching or playing sport?**

> Playing, definitely. I find watching sport on TV quite boring unless it's a really important match.

**5. Do you think people do enough exercise nowadays?**

> Generally not. Most people sit at a desk all day and then sit in front of a screen in the evening, so they don't move nearly enough.

---

## Useful phrases from these answers

- *switch off*
- *by far the most popular*
- *unless it's …*
- *not nearly enough*

## How to answer Part 1 well
- **Length:** 2–3 sentences. Answer directly, then add a reason, example or detail.
- **Formula:** *Answer → Why / Example → (Extra detail)*
- **Don't** memorise — examiners notice scripted answers. Use these models to collect *language*, then answer about your own life.
- Change the details (city, job, food) to match your life; keep the structures.
