# Model answers — Neighbours

**1. Do you know your neighbours?**

> I know a few of them by name, mostly the people on my floor. We chat in the lift, but I wouldn't say we're close friends.

**2. How often do you see them?**

> Almost every day, just in passing — when I'm leaving for work or taking the rubbish out.

**3. Have your neighbours ever helped you?**

> Yes, once when I was away on holiday, my neighbour watered my plants and took in a parcel for me. I bought her some chocolates to say thank you.

**4. Do people in your country usually know their neighbours?**

> In villages and small towns, definitely — everyone knows everyone. In big cities it's much less common, because people move frequently and are busy.

**5. What makes a good neighbour?**

> Someone who's friendly and considerate — who doesn't make too much noise late at night and is willing to help if you need something.

---

## Useful phrases from these answers

- *just in passing*
- *take in a parcel*
- *everyone knows everyone*
- *considerate*

## How to answer Part 1 well
- **Length:** 2–3 sentences. Answer directly, then add a reason, example or detail.
- **Formula:** *Answer → Why / Example → (Extra detail)*
- **Don't** memorise — examiners notice scripted answers. Use these models to collect *language*, then answer about your own life.
- Change the details (city, job, food) to match your life; keep the structures.
