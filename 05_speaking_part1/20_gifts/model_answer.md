# Model answers — Gifts

**1. Do you like giving gifts?**

> Yes, I really enjoy it, especially when I find something that suits the person perfectly. Seeing their reaction is the best part.

**2. What was the best gift you've received?**

> A handmade photo album my sister made for my twenty-fifth birthday, full of pictures from our childhood. It obviously meant a lot more than anything she could have bought.

**3. Is it difficult to choose gifts?**

> Sometimes, especially for people who seem to have everything already. In those cases I usually give an experience, like concert tickets, instead of an object.

**4. When do people give gifts in your country?**

> Mainly at festivals like Diwali and Onam, as well as at weddings and birthdays. At weddings, people often give money in an envelope.

**5. Do you think expensive gifts are better?**

> Not at all. The thought behind a gift matters far more than its price.

---

## Useful phrases from these answers

- *suits the person perfectly*
- *meant a lot more*
- *people who seem to have everything*
- *the thought behind it*

## How to answer Part 1 well
- **Length:** 2–3 sentences. Answer directly, then add a reason, example or detail.
- **Formula:** *Answer → Why / Example → (Extra detail)*
- **Don't** memorise — examiners notice scripted answers. Use these models to collect *language*, then answer about your own life.
- Change the details (city, job, food) to match your life; keep the structures.
