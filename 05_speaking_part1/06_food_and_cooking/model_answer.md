# Model answers — Food & Cooking

**1. What's your favourite food?**

> It has to be biryani — rice cooked with spices and meat or vegetables. Every region makes it slightly differently, and I enjoy comparing them.

**2. Do you like cooking?**

> I do, although I'm not particularly skilled. I can make a handful of simple dishes well, and I'm gradually trying to expand my repertoire.

**3. Do you prefer eating at home or eating out?**

> During the week I prefer eating at home because it's healthier and cheaper. But eating out at weekends is a nice treat, especially if I'm with friends.

**4. Is there any food you didn't like as a child but like now?**

> Bitter gourd! I used to refuse to eat it, but now I actually enjoy it, especially when it's fried with spices.

**5. Do people in your country eat healthily?**

> It's a mixed picture. Traditional home cooking is quite balanced, but fast food has become very popular among young people in cities, which is a concern.

---

## Useful phrases from these answers

- *It has to be …*
- *expand my repertoire*
- *a nice treat*
- *It's a mixed picture.*

## How to answer Part 1 well
- **Length:** 2–3 sentences. Answer directly, then add a reason, example or detail.
- **Formula:** *Answer → Why / Example → (Extra detail)*
- **Don't** memorise — examiners notice scripted answers. Use these models to collect *language*, then answer about your own life.
- Change the details (city, job, food) to match your life; keep the structures.
