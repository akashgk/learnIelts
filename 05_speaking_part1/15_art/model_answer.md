# Model answers — Art

**1. Do you like art?**

> I do, although I don't know much about it. I'm drawn to paintings with bright colours and landscapes rather than very abstract works.

**2. Have you ever been to an art gallery?**

> Yes, a few times. The most memorable was a modern art museum I visited in Singapore — some of the installations were really thought-provoking.

**3. Did you learn art at school?**

> Only in primary school. After that we had to choose between art and computing, and I chose computing, which I sometimes regret.

**4. Do you have any paintings or pictures at home?**

> Just a couple of framed prints in the living room — one of them is a map of my hometown, which reminds me of where I come from.

**5. Do you think art is important for children?**

> Absolutely. It encourages creativity and gives children a way to express feelings they might not be able to put into words.

---

## Useful phrases from these answers

- *I'm drawn to …*
- *thought-provoking*
- *which I sometimes regret*
- *put into words*

## How to answer Part 1 well
- **Length:** 2–3 sentences. Answer directly, then add a reason, example or detail.
- **Formula:** *Answer → Why / Example → (Extra detail)*
- **Don't** memorise — examiners notice scripted answers. Use these models to collect *language*, then answer about your own life.
- Change the details (city, job, food) to match your life; keep the structures.
