# Model answers — Transport & Travel

**1. How do you usually travel to work or school?**

> I take the metro. It's quick, air-conditioned and avoids the traffic, which can be terrible during rush hour.

**2. Is public transport good where you live?**

> It's improved a lot recently. The metro is excellent, but buses can be unreliable and very crowded.

**3. Do you like travelling?**

> I love it. Exploring new places, trying local food and meeting people from different backgrounds is one of my favourite things to do.

**4. Where did you go on your last trip?**

> I went to Sri Lanka last December with two friends. We travelled around by train through the tea plantations, which was spectacular.

**5. Where would you like to travel in the future?**

> Japan is at the top of my list. I'm fascinated by the mix of very traditional culture and cutting-edge technology.

---

## Useful phrases from these answers

- *during rush hour*
- *It's improved a lot recently*
- *at the top of my list*
- *cutting-edge*

## How to answer Part 1 well
- **Length:** 2–3 sentences. Answer directly, then add a reason, example or detail.
- **Formula:** *Answer → Why / Example → (Extra detail)*
- **Don't** memorise — examiners notice scripted answers. Use these models to collect *language*, then answer about your own life.
- Change the details (city, job, food) to match your life; keep the structures.
