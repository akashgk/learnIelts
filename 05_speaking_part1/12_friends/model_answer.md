# Model answers — Friends

**1. Do you have many friends?**

> I have a wide circle of acquaintances, but only a handful of really close friends — people I've known since school.

**2. How do you usually spend time with your friends?**

> We usually meet for dinner or play badminton at the weekend. With friends who live abroad, we keep in touch through video calls.

**3. Is it easy to make new friends as an adult?**

> It's harder than when you're a student, because people are busy with work and family. Joining clubs or classes definitely helps.

**4. What makes someone a good friend?**

> I'd say loyalty and honesty — someone who supports you but is also willing to tell you when you're making a mistake.

**5. Do you prefer having a few close friends or many friends?**

> A few close friends, for sure. I'd rather have deep friendships than lots of superficial ones.

---

## Useful phrases from these answers

- *a wide circle of acquaintances*
- *a handful of*
- *keep in touch*
- *I'd rather … than …*

## How to answer Part 1 well
- **Length:** 2–3 sentences. Answer directly, then add a reason, example or detail.
- **Formula:** *Answer → Why / Example → (Extra detail)*
- **Don't** memorise — examiners notice scripted answers. Use these models to collect *language*, then answer about your own life.
- Change the details (city, job, food) to match your life; keep the structures.
