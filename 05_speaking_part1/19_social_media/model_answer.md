# Model answers — Social Media

**1. Which social media do you use?**

> Mainly WhatsApp for keeping in touch with family, and Instagram, mostly to follow photographers and travel accounts.

**2. How much time do you spend on social media?**

> Probably about an hour a day, though it's easy to lose track. I've set a time limit on my phone to stop myself scrolling endlessly.

**3. What do you like about it?**

> I like that I can stay connected with friends who live abroad. It also helps me discover interesting things I'd never come across otherwise.

**4. Is there anything you dislike about it?**

> Yes — the way everyone presents a perfect version of their life. It can make you feel that everyone else is having more fun than you are.

**5. Do you think you'll use social media more or less in the future?**

> Less, I hope. I'd like to spend more time on hobbies that don't involve a screen.

---

## Useful phrases from these answers

- *keep in touch*
- *lose track (of time)*
- *scrolling endlessly*
- *come across*

## How to answer Part 1 well
- **Length:** 2–3 sentences. Answer directly, then add a reason, example or detail.
- **Formula:** *Answer → Why / Example → (Extra detail)*
- **Don't** memorise — examiners notice scripted answers. Use these models to collect *language*, then answer about your own life.
- Change the details (city, job, food) to match your life; keep the structures.
