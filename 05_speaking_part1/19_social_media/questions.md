# Speaking Part 1 — Social Media

*The examiner asks 4–5 questions on a familiar topic. Answer each in 2–3 sentences (about 15–25 seconds).*

1. Which social media do you use?
2. How much time do you spend on social media?
3. What do you like about it?
4. Is there anything you dislike about it?
5. Do you think you'll use social media more or less in the future?
