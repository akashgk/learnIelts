# Speaking Part 1 — Home & Accommodation

*The examiner asks 4–5 questions on a familiar topic. Answer each in 2–3 sentences (about 15–25 seconds).*

1. Do you live in a house or an apartment?
2. What's your favourite room?
3. What would you change about your home?
4. Do you plan to live there for a long time?
5. What kind of home would you like to live in in the future?
