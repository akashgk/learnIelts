# Model answers — Home & Accommodation

**1. Do you live in a house or an apartment?**

> I live in a two-bedroom apartment on the fifth floor of a fairly new building. I share it with a friend from university.

**2. What's your favourite room?**

> Definitely the living room. It has a big window facing west, so in the evening it gets lovely sunlight, and that's where I usually read or watch films.

**3. What would you change about your home?**

> If I could change one thing, I'd make the kitchen bigger. At the moment there's barely enough space for two people to cook at the same time.

**4. Do you plan to live there for a long time?**

> Probably not. It's great for now because it's close to my office, but eventually I'd like somewhere with a garden.

**5. What kind of home would you like to live in in the future?**

> Ideally, a small house on the edge of a city — somewhere quiet but still within easy reach of shops and public transport.

---

## Useful phrases from these answers

- *If I could change one thing, I'd …*
- *barely enough space*
- *within easy reach of*
- *Ideally, …*

## How to answer Part 1 well
- **Length:** 2–3 sentences. Answer directly, then add a reason, example or detail.
- **Formula:** *Answer → Why / Example → (Extra detail)*
- **Don't** memorise — examiners notice scripted answers. Use these models to collect *language*, then answer about your own life.
- Change the details (city, job, food) to match your life; keep the structures.
