# Speaking Part 1 — Work or Study

*The examiner asks 4–5 questions on a familiar topic. Answer each in 2–3 sentences (about 15–25 seconds).*

1. Do you work or are you a student?
2. Why did you choose that job / subject?
3. What do you like most about it?
4. Is there anything you dislike about it?
5. Would you like to change your job in the future?
