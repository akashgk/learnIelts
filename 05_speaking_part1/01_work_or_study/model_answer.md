# Model answers — Work or Study

**1. Do you work or are you a student?**

> I'm currently working as a software developer for a mid-sized company that builds apps for hospitals. I've been there for about two years, and before that I completed a degree in computer science.

**2. Why did you choose that job / subject?**

> Mainly because I've always enjoyed solving problems. When I was at school I loved maths puzzles, and programming felt like a natural extension of that. It also helps that there's a strong demand for developers, so the job prospects are good.

**3. What do you like most about it?**

> I'd say the sense of achievement when something finally works. There are days when I'm stuck on a bug for hours, but when I eventually fix it, it's incredibly satisfying.

**4. Is there anything you dislike about it?**

> To be honest, I spend more time in meetings than I'd like. Some of them are useful, but quite a few could probably have been an email.

**5. Would you like to change your job in the future?**

> Not completely, but I'd like to move into a more senior role where I can mentor junior colleagues. I think I'd enjoy the teaching side of it.

---

## Useful phrases from these answers

- *I've been … for (period)*
- *Mainly because …*
- *I'd say …*
- *To be honest, …*
- *Not completely, but …*

## How to answer Part 1 well
- **Length:** 2–3 sentences. Answer directly, then add a reason, example or detail.
- **Formula:** *Answer → Why / Example → (Extra detail)*
- **Don't** memorise — examiners notice scripted answers. Use these models to collect *language*, then answer about your own life.
- Change the details (city, job, food) to match your life; keep the structures.
