# Model answers — Mobile Phones & Technology

**1. How often do you use your phone?**

> Far more than I should! Probably several hours a day, between work messages, maps, music and social media.

**2. What do you mainly use it for?**

> Mostly for communication — messaging family and colleagues — and for navigation. I'd honestly be lost without map apps.

**3. Do you think people spend too much time on their phones?**

> Yes, I think many of us do. You often see a group of friends in a café all looking at their screens instead of talking to each other.

**4. Have you ever tried to use your phone less?**

> Yes, I've turned off most notifications and I don't keep my phone in the bedroom any more. It's made a noticeable difference to my sleep.

**5. What was the first phone you had?**

> A very basic one with buttons that could only make calls and send texts. I got it when I was fifteen, and I thought it was amazing at the time.

---

## Useful phrases from these answers

- *Far more than I should!*
- *I'd be lost without …*
- *a noticeable difference*

## How to answer Part 1 well
- **Length:** 2–3 sentences. Answer directly, then add a reason, example or detail.
- **Formula:** *Answer → Why / Example → (Extra detail)*
- **Don't** memorise — examiners notice scripted answers. Use these models to collect *language*, then answer about your own life.
- Change the details (city, job, food) to match your life; keep the structures.
