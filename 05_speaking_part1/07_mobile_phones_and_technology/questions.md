# Speaking Part 1 — Mobile Phones & Technology

*The examiner asks 4–5 questions on a familiar topic. Answer each in 2–3 sentences (about 15–25 seconds).*

1. How often do you use your phone?
2. What do you mainly use it for?
3. Do you think people spend too much time on their phones?
4. Have you ever tried to use your phone less?
5. What was the first phone you had?
