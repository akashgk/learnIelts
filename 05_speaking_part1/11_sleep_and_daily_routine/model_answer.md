# Model answers — Sleep & Daily Routine

**1. How many hours do you usually sleep?**

> Around seven hours on weeknights, which is probably slightly less than I need. At weekends I tend to catch up.

**2. Are you a morning person or a night person?**

> Definitely a morning person. I'm at my most productive before lunch, and by ten at night I'm ready for bed.

**3. What do you do to relax before sleeping?**

> I try to avoid screens for half an hour and read instead. A cup of herbal tea helps too.

**4. Do you take naps during the day?**

> Rarely on weekdays, because I'm at work, but I do enjoy a short nap on Sunday afternoons.

**5. Is your daily routine different at weekends?**

> Completely. During the week everything is planned around work, but at weekends I prefer to have no fixed schedule at all.

---

## Useful phrases from these answers

- *catch up (on sleep)*
- *at my most productive*
- *Rarely …, but …*
- *no fixed schedule*

## How to answer Part 1 well
- **Length:** 2–3 sentences. Answer directly, then add a reason, example or detail.
- **Formula:** *Answer → Why / Example → (Extra detail)*
- **Don't** memorise — examiners notice scripted answers. Use these models to collect *language*, then answer about your own life.
- Change the details (city, job, food) to match your life; keep the structures.
