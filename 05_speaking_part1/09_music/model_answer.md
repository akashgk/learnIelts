# Model answers — Music

**1. What kind of music do you like?**

> I have quite eclectic taste, but I mostly listen to indie rock and some classical Indian music, which my grandfather introduced me to.

**2. When do you usually listen to music?**

> Mainly when I'm commuting or exercising. I find it helps me concentrate, as long as there are no lyrics when I'm working.

**3. Can you play a musical instrument?**

> Unfortunately not. I had keyboard lessons as a child but gave up after a year, which I now slightly regret.

**4. Have your music tastes changed over time?**

> Yes, a lot. As a teenager I only listened to pop, but now I'm much more open to different genres.

**5. Do you prefer live music or recordings?**

> Live music, without a doubt. The atmosphere at a concert is something you can't recreate with headphones.

---

## Useful phrases from these answers

- *eclectic taste*
- *as long as …*
- *which I now slightly regret*
- *without a doubt*

## How to answer Part 1 well
- **Length:** 2–3 sentences. Answer directly, then add a reason, example or detail.
- **Formula:** *Answer → Why / Example → (Extra detail)*
- **Don't** memorise — examiners notice scripted answers. Use these models to collect *language*, then answer about your own life.
- Change the details (city, job, food) to match your life; keep the structures.
