## Questions 1–5 · True / False / Not Given

**1. TRUE** — Paragraph A: 'Roughly three-quarters … depend, at least in part, on animals to carry pollen'.

**2. FALSE** — Paragraph B: 'The majority of bee species … are wild and solitary'.

**3. FALSE** — Paragraph C: the total 'has actually risen over the same period'.
  
  *Trap:* The heavy winter losses tempt you to say TRUE — but they were replaced.

**4. TRUE** — Paragraph D: 'led the European Union to ban their outdoor use in 2018'.

**5. NOT GIVEN** — The passage says it 'spread around the world', but not where it came from.

## Questions 6–9 · Completion (sentence / note / table / form / summary)

**6. stems** — Paragraph B: 'in hollow plant stems'.

**7. splitting** — Paragraph C: 'by splitting healthy ones'.

**8. bloom** — Paragraph D: 'some plants now bloom before the bees … have emerged'.

**9. wildflowers** — Paragraph F: 'strips of wildflowers along the edges of their fields'.

## Questions 10–13 · Multiple choice (one answer)

**10. B** — 'responsible for a much larger share of crop pollination than was once believed'.

**11. A** — Paragraph C: 'since nobody is available to replace them'.

**12. B** — A poorly fed bee is 'more vulnerable to disease' — stresses combine.

**13. B** — Paragraph F: hives 'can compete with wild bees for the limited flowers available'.
