## Questions 1–5 · Matching (headings, information, features, endings)

**1. A** — 'The word "placebo" comes from Latin and means "I shall please".'

**2. D** — Endorphins — 'natural painkilling chemicals' — are released.

**3. C** — Natural cycles and 'regression to the mean' 'can easily be mistaken for a placebo response'.

**4. E** — 'warm, attentive practitioner' vs 'polite but brief'.

**5. B** — The controlled trial procedure is described step by step.

## Questions 6–9 · True / False / Not Given

**6. FALSE** — Paragraph C: the effect is 'largely confined to symptoms that patients report themselves … rather than to measurable conditions such as tumour size'.

**7. TRUE** — Paragraph D: 'the placebo effect is significantly reduced'.

**8. NOT GIVEN** — Age is never mentioned.

**9. TRUE** — Paragraph E: 'branded tablets outperform those in plain packaging' — the same idea expressed the other way round.
  
  *Trap:* Paraphrase reversal: 'A outperforms B' = 'B is weaker than A'.

## Questions 10–13 · Short answer

**10. 1955** — Paragraph B.

**11. a third, around a third, one third** — Paragraph B: 'around a third of patients'.

**12. natural cycles** — Paragraph C: 'illnesses follow natural cycles'. Note that 'regression to the mean' (four words) would break the three-word limit — always count.

**13. open-label, open-label placebos** — Paragraph F: 'these "open-label" results'. Hyphenated words count as one word.
