## Questions 1–5 · Completion (sentence / note / table / form / summary)

> **Strategy:** Flow-charts describe a process or sequence in order. Follow the arrows and keep track of where you are in the passage.

**1. Californian** — 'a Californian supermarket'. Copy the word exactly as it appears — you may not change 'Californian' into 'California'.

**2. 24, twenty-four** — '24 varieties of jam'.

**3. taste** — 'attracted more passers-by to stop and taste'.

**4. 30 per cent, 30 percent, 30%** — 'around 30 per cent of shoppers who had seen the small selection'.

**5. motivation** — 'could reduce people's motivation to choose at all'.

## Questions 6–9 · Matching (headings, information, features, endings)

**6. B** — 'satisficers … are content to select the first option that meets their requirements'.

**7. A** — 'maximisers often make objectively better choices'.

**8. B** — Maximisers 'report feeling less satisfied … than satisficers do'.

**9. A** — Maximisers 'feel they must examine every possibility'.

## Questions 10–13 · Multiple choice (one answer)

**10. B** — 'the average effect of choice overload across all of them was close to zero'.
  
  *Trap:* A — the writer says 'This does not necessarily mean the original finding was wrong.'

**11. C** — 'when the choice must be made quickly'. A, B and D all reduce overload.

**12. B** — One 'may enjoy the variety'; the other 'may find the same shelf paralysing'.

**13. D** — Final paragraph: 'the question is not simply "how many options?" but "how can people be helped to navigate them?"'
