## Questions 1–4 · Multiple choice (one answer)

**1. B** — They 'compared children from poor immigrant families with wealthier native speakers'; the disadvantage vanished when researchers 'control[led] for social background'.
  
  *Trap:* C is the opposite: they were tested in their WEAKER language.

**2. B** — 'must constantly suppress the language that is not needed'.

**3. A** — 'allows the brain to cope with damage for longer before function declines'.

**4. B** — 'experiments that find an interesting effect are more likely to be … accepted'.
