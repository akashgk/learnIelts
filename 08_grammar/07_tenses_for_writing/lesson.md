# Grammar — Tenses for Task 1 and Task 2

*Choose the right tense for past data, present facts, changes up to now and future predictions.*

## Task 1
| Data | Tense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Past years only | past simple | *In 2010, sales **rose**.* |
| From past to now / map changes | present perfect | *The park **has been** extended.* |
| By a past point | past perfect | *By 2015, numbers **had doubled**.* |
| Projections | future / *is expected to* | *Sales **are predicted to** reach…* |
| No time given (process) | present simple | *The water **is filtered**.* |

## Task 2
- **General truths:** present simple — *Technology **affects** every part of life.*
- **Recent trends:** present perfect / continuous — *More people **have started** working from home.* / *are increasingly choosing*
- **Predictions:** *will, is likely to, may well*
