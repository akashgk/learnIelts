# Tenses for Task 1 and Task 2 — Questions

## Questions 1–8

*Choose the correct letter, A, B or C.*

**1.** In 2005, the figure ____ to 190,000.

- **A** has risen
- **B** rose
- **C** rises

**2.** By 2020, the number of users ____ fourfold.

- **A** increased
- **B** has increased
- **C** had increased

**3.** Since 1995, the factory ____ into flats.

- **A** was converted
- **B** has been converted
- **C** converts

**4.** Sales ____ to reach 5 million by 2030.

- **A** are predicted
- **B** predicted
- **C** had predicted

**5.** Over the past decade, online shopping ____ enormously.

- **A** grew
- **B** has grown
- **C** had grown

**6.** Once the leaves ____, they are dried.

- **A** were picked
- **B** have been picked
- **C** had picked

**7.** Nowadays, more young people ____ to live alone.

- **A** are choosing
- **B** chose
- **C** had chosen

**8.** In 1990, Germany ____ the leading destination.

- **A** is
- **B** has been
- **C** was
