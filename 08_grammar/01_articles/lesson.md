# Grammar — Articles: a, an, the, Ø

*When to use a/an, the, or no article — the most common error for many IELTS candidates.*

## Rules you need for IELTS

| Use | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **a / an** | first mention of a singular countable noun; one of many | *She bought **a** laptop.* |
| **the** | something already mentioned or known; unique things; superlatives; specific groups | ***The** laptop was expensive.* · ***the** internet* · ***the** highest figure* · ***the** elderly* |
| **Ø (no article)** | general statements with plural or uncountable nouns | ***Ø** Technology changes **Ø** people's lives.* |
| **the** + chart data | specific categories in a chart | ***The** number of students rose.* · ***The** proportion of women…* |

### High-frequency IELTS patterns
- ✅ *the number of / the percentage of / the amount of* (always **the**)
- ✅ *in the 1990s*, *in the UK*, *the USA*, but *in Ø France*, *in Ø Japan*
- ✅ *Ø Education is important.* (general) vs *the education of girls* (specific)
- ❌ *The pollution is a big problem.* → ✅ *Pollution is a big problem.* (general, uncountable)
