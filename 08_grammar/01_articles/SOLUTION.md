## Questions 1–8 · Multiple choice (one answer)

**1. B** — 'The number of' is a fixed pattern.

**2. C** — General statement with an uncountable noun → no article.

**3. B** — Superlatives take 'the'.

**4. A** — 'The elderly' = a group of people (the + adjective).

**5. B** — Jobs take a/an; 'engineer' starts with a vowel sound → an.

**6. A** — Decades take 'the'.

**7. C** — General statement — no article.

**8. B** — Countries with 'Kingdom', 'States', 'Republic' take 'the'.
