# Grammar — Quantifiers: much, many, few, little, a number of

*Use quantifiers correctly with countable and uncountable nouns.*

## Countable vs uncountable
| With countable plurals | With uncountable nouns | With both |
|---|---|---|
| *many, a few, few, fewer, a number of, several* | *much, a little, little, less, an amount of, a great deal of* | *some, any, a lot of, lots of, plenty of, most, all, no* |

## few / a few, little / a little
- *a few / a little* = some (positive): ***A few** students passed.*
- *few / little* = almost none (negative): ***Few** students passed.* · *There is **little** evidence.*

## Formal writing
- Prefer *many / much / a large number of / a great deal of* to *a lot of*.
- *much* is mainly used in negatives and questions: *There is **not much** time.*
