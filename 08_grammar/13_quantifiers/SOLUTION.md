## Questions 1–10 · Multiple choice (one answer)

**2. B** — Many failed → disappointed. 'Few' would mean almost none failed.

**3. A** — little = almost none (uncountable).

**4. B** — people are countable → number.

**5. A** — information is uncountable; 'more' works for both.

**6. B** — accidents are countable → fewer.

**7. B** — a few = some (positive meaning).

**8. B** — a great deal of + uncountable.

**9. B** — furniture is uncountable.

**10. A** — most of + plural.
