# Quantifiers: much, many, few, little, a number of — Questions

## Questions 1–10

*Choose the correct letter, A, B or C.*

**1.** There isn't ____ evidence to support this claim.

- **A** many
- **B** much
- **C** a few

**2.** ____ students failed, so the teacher was disappointed. (almost none passed)

- **A** Few
- **B** Many
- **C** Little

**3.** There is ____ hope of finding survivors. (almost no hope)

- **A** little
- **B** a little
- **C** few

**4.** A large ____ of people attended.

- **A** amount
- **B** number
- **C** deal

**5.** We need ____ information before deciding.

- **A** more
- **B** many
- **C** a few

**6.** The new law led to ____ accidents.

- **A** less
- **B** fewer
- **C** little

**7.** I have ____ friends in this city, so I'm not lonely.

- **A** few
- **B** a few
- **C** a little

**8.** A great ____ of money was wasted.

- **A** number
- **B** deal
- **C** many

**9.** How ____ furniture did they buy?

- **A** many
- **B** much
- **C** few

**10.** ____ of the participants agreed with the proposal.

- **A** Most
- **B** Much
- **C** Little
