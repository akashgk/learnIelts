# Grammar — Nominalisation & Noun Phrases

*Turn verbs and adjectives into nouns to write in a concise, academic style.*

## What is nominalisation?
Turning an action (verb) or quality (adjective) into a **noun**, which lets you pack more information into a sentence.

| Less academic | More academic |
|---|---|
| *Prices **increased**, which **worried** consumers.* | *The **increase** in prices caused consumer **concern**.* |
| *If we **invest** in education, the economy **grows**.* | ***Investment** in education promotes economic **growth**.* |
| *People are **aware** that…* | *There is growing **awareness** that…* |

## Common pairs
*increase → increase · decline → decline · reduce → reduction · develop → development · expand → expansion · fail → failure · able → ability · important → importance · available → availability · urbanise → urbanisation*

## Long noun phrases
*the rapid **growth** of online shopping in developing countries* — head noun + adjective + prepositional phrase.

⚠️ Don't overdo it. One or two nominalisations per sentence keeps writing readable.
