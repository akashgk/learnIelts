# Nominalisation & Noun Phrases — Questions

## Questions 1–10

*Choose the correct letter, A, B or C.*

**1.** Nominalise: 'Unemployment fell sharply.' → 'There was a sharp ____ in unemployment.'

- **A** fall
- **B** falling
- **C** fell

**2.** 'reduce' → noun:

- **A** reducement
- **B** reduction
- **C** reducing

**3.** 'expand' → noun:

- **A** expansion
- **B** expandment
- **C** expanse

**4.** 'available' → noun:

- **A** availableness
- **B** availability
- **C** avail

**5.** Which sentence is most academic?

- **A** Because cities grew fast, there were problems.
- **B** Rapid urban growth created a range of problems.
- **C** Cities grew fast and it was a problem.

**6.** 'important' → noun:

- **A** importance
- **B** importancy
- **C** importantness

**7.** Complete: 'The ____ of renewable energy has reduced emissions.' (develop)

- **A** develop
- **B** developing
- **C** development

**8.** 'fail' → noun:

- **A** failment
- **B** failure
- **C** failing

**9.** Complete: 'There is growing ____ of climate change.' (aware)

- **A** aware
- **B** awareness
- **C** awarely

**10.** Which noun phrase is correctly formed?

- **A** the rapid growth of online shopping
- **B** the rapidly growth of online shopping
- **C** the rapid grow of online shopping
