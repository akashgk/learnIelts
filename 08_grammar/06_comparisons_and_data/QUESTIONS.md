# Comparing Data & Numbers — Questions

## Questions 1–8

*Choose the correct letter, A, B or C.*

**1.** London's pass was ____ expensive than Berlin's.

- **A** more
- **B** much more
- **C** most

**2.** Tokyo had ____ highest rate of use.

- **A** the
- **B** a
- **C** more

**3.** There were ____ visitors in 2020 than in 2018.

- **A** less
- **B** fewer
- **C** lower

**4.** The ____ of tourists fell.

- **A** amount
- **B** number
- **C** quantity of much

**5.** Sales in 2020 were three times ____ high as in 2000.

- **A** as
- **B** than
- **C** so

**6.** 49% is best described as:

- **A** just over half
- **B** just under half
- **C** a quarter

**7.** Spending on health rose from 4% to 8%. It ____.

- **A** doubled
- **B** tripled
- **C** halved

**8.** Coal was ____ the largest source of energy.

- **A** by far
- **B** very
- **C** more
