# Grammar — Comparing Data & Numbers

*Accurate comparatives, superlatives, fractions and approximations for Task 1.*

## Comparatives & superlatives
- *higher than*, *the highest*, *more popular than*, *the most popular*
- *twice as many as*, *three times as much as*, *half as many as*
- *significantly / considerably / slightly / marginally higher*
- *by far the largest*

## Numbers
| Data | Language |
|---|---|
| 49% | just under half / almost half / nearly half |
| 51% | just over half / a slight majority |
| 24% | roughly a quarter / approximately one in four |
| 3× | tripled / three times as high |
| doubled | rose twofold / doubled |

## *many/much, fewer/less*
- **Countable:** *many people, fewer students, a number of cars*
- **Uncountable:** *much money, less water, an amount of energy*

### Mistakes to avoid
- ❌ *more higher* → ✅ *higher*
- ❌ *less people* (formal writing) → ✅ *fewer people*
- ❌ *the amount of tourists* → ✅ *the number of tourists*
