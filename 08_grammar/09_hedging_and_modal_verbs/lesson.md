# Grammar — Hedging & Modal Verbs

*Soften claims like an academic writer: may, might, could, tend to, it is likely that.*

## Why hedge?
Band 7+ Task 2 essays avoid over-generalising. *"Technology makes people lonely"* is too absolute; *"Technology **may** make **some** people lonelier"* is precise and defensible.

| Tool | Examples |
|---|---|
| Modal verbs | *may, might, could, can* |
| Verbs | *tend to, appear to, seem to, suggest* |
| Adverbs | *possibly, probably, arguably, generally, largely* |
| Quantifiers | *some, many, most, a significant proportion of* |
| Impersonal phrases | *It is likely that…, It could be argued that…, There is evidence that…* |

### Strength scale
*will* (certain) → *is likely to* → *may/might/could* (possible) → *is unlikely to*

### Don't over-hedge
❌ *It could possibly perhaps be argued that maybe…* — one hedge per claim is enough.
