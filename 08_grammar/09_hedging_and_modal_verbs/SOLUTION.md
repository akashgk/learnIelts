## Questions 1–10 · Multiple choice (one answer)

**1. B** — Modal + 'contribute to' + 'some' — precise and defensible.

**2. A** — 'tend to + verb' expresses a general pattern.

**3. A** — 'It could be argued that…' presents a view tentatively.

**4. B** — 'will' expresses certainty.

**5. A** — 'There is evidence that + clause'.

**6. A** — Arguably = a reasonable claim, but open to debate.

**7. A** — 'is likely to + verb'. 'Probably' is an adverb: 'will probably reduce'.

**8. B** — Stacking hedges sounds unnatural.

**9. A** — 'appear to + verb'.

**10. C** — 'many' avoids an absolute claim.
