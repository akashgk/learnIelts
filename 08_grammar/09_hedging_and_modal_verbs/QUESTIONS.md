# Hedging & Modal Verbs — Questions

## Questions 1–10

*Choose the correct letter, A, B or C.*

**1.** Which sentence is best hedged for an academic essay?

- **A** Video games cause violence.
- **B** Video games may contribute to aggressive behaviour in some children.
- **C** Video games always make children violent.

**2.** Young people ____ to spend more time online than older adults.

- **A** tend
- **B** are tend
- **C** tending

**3.** It ____ be argued that tourism damages local culture.

- **A** could
- **B** must
- **C** will

**4.** Which is the strongest (most certain) prediction?

- **A** Prices may rise.
- **B** Prices will rise.
- **C** Prices might rise.

**5.** There is evidence ____ exercise improves memory.

- **A** that
- **B** which
- **C** for

**6.** 'Arguably' means:

- **A** it can reasonably be claimed
- **B** it is certainly true
- **C** nobody agrees

**7.** The new policy is ____ to reduce traffic. (probable result)

- **A** likely
- **B** probably
- **C** possible

**8.** Which sentence over-hedges?

- **A** This may reduce costs.
- **B** This could perhaps possibly maybe reduce costs.
- **C** This is likely to reduce costs.

**9.** Choose the correct form: 'Smartphones ____ to be making teenagers less sociable.'

- **A** appear
- **B** appearing
- **C** are appear

**10.** Which quantifier avoids over-generalising?

- **A** all students
- **B** every student
- **C** many students
