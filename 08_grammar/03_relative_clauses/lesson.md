# Grammar — Relative Clauses & Complex Sentences

*Combine ideas with who/which/that/where/whose to raise your Grammatical Range score.*

## Defining vs non-defining

| Type | Purpose | Commas? | *that* allowed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Defining** | tells us *which one* | No | Yes |
| **Non-defining** | adds extra information | **Yes** | **No** |

- Defining: *Students **who** work part-time often have less time to study.*
- Non-defining: *My brother, **who** lives in Canada, is a nurse.*
- Comment on a whole clause: *Many young people live alone, **which** can lead to loneliness.*

### Relative words
*who* (people) · *which* (things) · *that* (people/things, defining only) · *whose* (possession) · *where* (places) · *when* (times) · *in which / by which* (formal)

### Reduced relative clauses (Band 7+)
- *The figures **shown** in the table…* (= which are shown)
- *People **living** in cities…* (= who live)
