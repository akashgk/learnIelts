# Relative Clauses & Complex Sentences — Questions

## Questions 1–8

*Choose the correct letter, A, B or C.*

**1.** Australia, ____ attracted 330,000 students, was the top destination.

- **A** that
- **B** which
- **C** who

**2.** People ____ exercise regularly tend to live longer.

- **A** who
- **B** which
- **C** whose

**3.** The town ____ I grew up has changed a lot.

- **A** which
- **B** where
- **C** when

**4.** Children ____ parents read to them develop better language skills.

- **A** who
- **B** whose
- **C** which

**5.** Many students work part-time, ____ reduces their study time.

- **A** what
- **B** which
- **C** that

**6.** The year ____ the factory closed was 1998.

- **A** when
- **B** where
- **C** which

**7.** The data ____ in the chart covers ten years. (reduced clause)

- **A** showing
- **B** shown
- **C** shows

**8.** People ____ in rural areas often lack healthcare. (reduced clause)

- **A** lived
- **B** living
- **C** live
