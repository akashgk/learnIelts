## Questions 1–8 · Multiple choice (one answer)

**1. B** — Non-defining clause (with commas) → 'which', never 'that'.

**2. A** — People → who.

**3. B** — Place + clause with its own subject → where (= in which).

**4. B** — Possession → whose.

**5. B** — 'which' can refer to the whole previous clause.

**6. A** — Time → when.

**7. B** — Passive meaning (the data is shown) → past participle 'shown'.

**8. B** — Active meaning (people who live) → present participle 'living'.
