# Grammar — Emphasis: Cleft Sentences & Inversion

*Add emphasis and variety with 'What … is', 'It is … that' and negative inversion.*

## Cleft sentences
- *Pollution worries me.* → ***What** worries me **is** pollution.*
- *Governments must act.* → ***It is** governments **that** must act.*
- *The cost is the main problem.* → ***The main problem is** the cost.*

## Negative inversion (formal, Band 8+)
After a negative or limiting expression at the start, use **auxiliary + subject**:
- ***Not only does** tourism create jobs, **but** it also funds conservation.*
- ***Rarely have** we seen such rapid change.*
- ***Only by** investing in education **can** we reduce poverty.*
- ***Never before has** information been so accessible.*

⚠️ Use sparingly — once or twice in an essay is impressive; more sounds unnatural.
