# Sentence Boundaries & Punctuation — Questions

## Questions 1–10

*Choose the correct letter, A, B or C.*

**1.** Which sentence is correct?

- **A** Prices rose, people bought less.
- **B** Prices rose, so people bought less.
- **C** Prices rose so, people bought less.

**2.** Which is a sentence fragment?

- **A** Because the rent was too high.
- **B** They moved because the rent was too high.
- **C** The rent was too high.

**3.** Choose the correctly punctuated sentence:

- **A** The plan was expensive; however, it was approved.
- **B** The plan was expensive, however it was approved.
- **C** The plan was expensive however, it was approved.

**4.** Choose the correct sentence:

- **A** There are three reasons: cost, time and safety.
- **B** There are three reasons; cost, time and safety.
- **C** There are three reasons, cost: time and safety.

**5.** Which needs NO comma?

- **A** Students who study abroad gain confidence.
- **B** My sister, who studies abroad, is confident.
- **C** However, studying abroad is expensive.

**6.** Correct the comma splice: 'Cities are growing, housing is scarce.'

- **A** Cities are growing, and housing is scarce.
- **B** Cities are growing housing is scarce.
- **C** Cities are growing, housing, is scarce.

**7.** Choose the correct sentence:

- **A** Although it rained, the match continued.
- **B** Although it rained the match, continued.
- **C** Although, it rained the match continued.

**8.** Which is correct?

- **A** He argued, that taxes should rise.
- **B** He argued that taxes should rise.
- **C** He argued that, taxes should rise.

**9.** Which sentence is a run-on (no punctuation between clauses)?

- **A** Fees rose students protested.
- **B** Fees rose, and students protested.
- **C** When fees rose, students protested.

**10.** Choose the best punctuation: 'Two factors matter most ___ price and location.'

- **A** :
- **B** ;
- **C** ,
