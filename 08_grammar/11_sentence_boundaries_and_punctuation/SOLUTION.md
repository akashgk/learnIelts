## Questions 1–10 · Multiple choice (one answer)

**1. B** — Join two clauses with a conjunction (so), not just a comma.

**2. A** — A 'because' clause alone is incomplete.

**3. A** — Semicolon before mid-sentence 'however', comma after it.

**4. A** — Colon introduces a list.

**5. A** — Defining relative clause → no commas.

**6. A** — Add a conjunction (and).

**7. A** — Comma after the introductory clause.

**8. B** — No comma before a that-clause.

**9. A** — Two clauses with no linker or punctuation.

**10. A** — Colon introduces an explanation/list.
