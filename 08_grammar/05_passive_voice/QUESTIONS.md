# The Passive Voice — Questions

## Questions 1–8

*Choose the correct letter, A, B or C.*

**1.** First, the cocoa beans ____ from the trees.

- **A** pick
- **B** are picked
- **C** are picking

**2.** Since 2000, the old station ____ into a museum.

- **A** has converted
- **B** has been converted
- **C** was converting

**3.** A new hospital ____ next year.

- **A** will be built
- **B** will build
- **C** is building

**4.** Unemployment ____ sharply in 2009.

- **A** was risen
- **B** rose
- **C** was rose

**5.** It ____ that technology will replace many jobs.

- **A** is often argued
- **B** often argues
- **C** is often arguing

**6.** The data ____ by a government agency last year.

- **A** collected
- **B** was collected
- **C** has collected

**7.** The bottles are crushed and then ____ into flakes.

- **A** shred
- **B** shredded
- **C** shredding

**8.** More money should ____ on education.

- **A** spend
- **B** be spent
- **C** been spent
