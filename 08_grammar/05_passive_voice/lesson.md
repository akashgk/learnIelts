# Grammar — The Passive Voice

*Essential for process diagrams, maps and formal academic style.*

## Form: **be + past participle**

| Tense | Active | Passive |
|---|---|---|
| Present simple | Workers sort the bottles. | The bottles **are sorted**. |
| Past simple | They built a bridge. | A bridge **was built**. |
| Present perfect | They have demolished the factory. | The factory **has been demolished**. |
| Future | They will add a café. | A café **will be added**. |
| Modal | We should reduce taxes. | Taxes **should be reduced**. |

### When to use in IELTS
- **Process diagrams:** *The leaves are picked, dried and then crushed.*
- **Maps:** *The park has been replaced by a car park.*
- **Task 2 formality:** *It is widely believed that…*, *It could be argued that…*

### Common mistake
Intransitive verbs have **no passive**: ❌ *The number was increased.* (if nobody increased it) → ✅ *The number increased.* — *rise, fall, happen, occur, remain* are never passive.
