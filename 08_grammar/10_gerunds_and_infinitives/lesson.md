# Grammar — Gerunds & Infinitives

*Choose -ing or to + verb after common verbs, adjectives and prepositions.*

## Verb + -ing
*enjoy, avoid, consider, suggest, recommend, admit, deny, involve, risk, mind, finish, keep, practise*
- *Experts **recommend reducing** sugar intake.*

## Verb + to-infinitive
*want, decide, plan, hope, agree, refuse, aim, manage, fail, afford, tend, seem, promise*
- *The government **aims to cut** emissions.*

## Preposition + -ing (always)
- *interested **in learning**, responsible **for managing**, instead **of driving**, **by investing***
- ⚠️ *look forward **to seeing***, *be used **to working***, *object **to paying*** — here *to* is a preposition.

## Both, with a change of meaning
| | -ing | to + verb |
|---|---|---|
| **stop** | *stopped smoking* (quit) | *stopped to smoke* (paused in order to) |
| **remember** | *remember meeting* (past memory) | *remember to lock* (future task) |
| **try** | *try adding salt* (experiment) | *try to finish* (make an effort) |
