# Gerunds & Infinitives — Questions

## Questions 1–10

*Choose the correct letter, A, B or C.*

**1.** Many people avoid ____ public transport at night.

- **A** using
- **B** to use
- **C** use

**2.** The council has decided ____ a new park.

- **A** building
- **B** to build
- **C** build

**3.** Governments can reduce traffic by ____ public transport.

- **A** improve
- **B** to improve
- **C** improving

**4.** I look forward to ____ from you.

- **A** hear
- **B** hearing
- **C** heard

**5.** Doctors recommend ____ at least seven hours a night.

- **A** sleeping
- **B** to sleep
- **C** sleep

**6.** He stopped ____ because it was bad for his health. (= quit)

- **A** to smoke
- **B** smoking
- **C** smoke

**7.** Remember ____ the door when you leave.

- **A** locking
- **B** to lock
- **C** lock

**8.** Many families cannot afford ____ abroad.

- **A** travelling
- **B** to travel
- **C** travel

**9.** Instead of ____ by car, try cycling.

- **A** going
- **B** to go
- **C** go

**10.** She is used to ____ long hours.

- **A** work
- **B** working
- **C** worked
