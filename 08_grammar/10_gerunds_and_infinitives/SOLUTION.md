## Questions 1–10 · Multiple choice (one answer)

**1. A** — avoid + -ing.

**2. B** — decide + to-infinitive.

**3. C** — Preposition + -ing.

**4. B** — 'to' here is a preposition → -ing.

**5. A** — recommend + -ing.

**6. B** — stop + -ing = quit the activity.

**7. B** — remember + to-infinitive = a task to do.

**8. B** — afford + to-infinitive.

**9. A** — instead of + -ing.

**10. B** — be used to + -ing (accustomed to).
