# Grammar — Common Errors: Countable Nouns, Prepositions, Word Forms

*Fix the small errors that pull Band 6 scripts down.*

## Uncountable nouns (no *a*, no plural)
*advice · information · research · evidence · knowledge · equipment · furniture · traffic · accommodation · homework · progress · feedback*
- ❌ *an advice / advices* → ✅ *a piece of advice / some advice*
- ❌ *researches* → ✅ *research / studies*

## Dependent prepositions
| ✅ Correct | ❌ Wrong |
|---|---|
| *have an effect **on*** | effect to |
| *responsible **for*** | responsible of |
| *depend **on*** | depend of |
| *an increase **in** sales* / *increase **by** 10%* | increase of sales |
| *access **to*** | access for |
| *emphasise X* / *put emphasis **on** X* | emphasise on X |
| *discuss X* | discuss about X |

## Word forms
*economy (n) / economic (adj: related to the economy) / economical (adj: saves money)*
*benefit (n,v) / beneficial (adj)* · *effect (n) / affect (v)*
