# Common Errors: Countable Nouns, Prepositions, Word Forms — Questions

## Questions 1–8

*Choose the correct letter, A, B or C.*

**1.** Can you give me some ____ about the exam?

- **A** advices
- **B** advice
- **C** an advice

**2.** Smartphones have a negative effect ____ sleep.

- **A** on
- **B** to
- **C** for

**3.** Parents are responsible ____ their children's safety.

- **A** of
- **B** for
- **C** about

**4.** The essay discusses ____ the causes of crime.

- **A** about
- **B** on
- **C** Ø (no word)

**5.** Pollution can ____ people's health.

- **A** effect
- **B** affect
- **C** affection

**6.** The country is going through an ____ crisis.

- **A** economical
- **B** economic
- **C** economy

**7.** There was a sharp increase ____ unemployment.

- **A** of
- **B** in
- **C** on

**8.** Exercise is ____ for mental health.

- **A** benefit
- **B** beneficial
- **C** benefited
