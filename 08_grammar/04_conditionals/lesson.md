# Grammar — Conditionals for Arguments

*Use conditionals to suggest solutions and predict consequences in Task 2 and Speaking.*

## The four you need

| Type | Form | Use in IELTS | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zero** | if + present, present | general truths | *If people sleep less, their concentration suffers.* |
| **First** | if + present, will/can | realistic future results | *If governments tax sugar, consumption will fall.* |
| **Second** | if + past, would/could | hypothetical proposals | *If public transport were free, more people would use it.* |
| **Third** | if + past perfect, would have | past regrets / analysis | *If I had started earlier, I would have scored higher.* |

### Alternatives to *if* (great for range)
*unless* · *provided (that)* · *as long as* · *otherwise* · *Were the government to…, …* (inversion, formal) · *Without X, Y would…*
