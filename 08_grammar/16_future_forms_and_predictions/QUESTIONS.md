# Future Forms & Predictions — Questions

## Questions 1–10

*Choose the correct letter, A, B or C.*

**1.** By 2050, the world's population ____ to nearly 10 billion.

- **A** will have grown
- **B** will grow up
- **C** has grown

**2.** When the new bridge ____, journeys will be shorter.

- **A** will open
- **B** opens
- **C** is opening

**3.** Sales ____ to rise next year, according to analysts.

- **A** are expected
- **B** expect
- **C** are expecting

**4.** This time next week, I ____ on a beach.

- **A** will lie
- **B** will be lying
- **C** lie

**5.** Look at those dark clouds — it ____ rain.

- **A** will
- **B** is going to
- **C** rains

**6.** I ____ my tutor at 3 p.m. tomorrow; we've arranged it.

- **A** meet
- **B** am meeting
- **C** will have met

**7.** The train ____ at 6.15 tomorrow morning.

- **A** leaves
- **B** will be leaving
- **C** is going to leave

**8.** Electric cars are ____ to replace petrol cars eventually.

- **A** likely
- **B** probably
- **C** possibly

**9.** As soon as I ____ my results, I'll call you.

- **A** will get
- **B** get
- **C** am getting

**10.** By the time you arrive, we ____ dinner.

- **A** will finish
- **B** will have finished
- **C** finish
