# Grammar — Future Forms & Predictions

*Express predictions, plans and probability about the future accurately.*

## Forms
| Form | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *will* | prediction, opinion | *Technology **will** change education.* |
| *be going to* | prediction from evidence; plan | *Look at the clouds — it's **going to** rain.* |
| *be likely / set / expected to* | formal prediction | *Prices **are expected to** rise.* |
| *will be + -ing* | action in progress at a future time | *By 2030, people **will be working** from home more.* |
| *will have + past participle* | completed before a future time | *By 2050, the population **will have doubled**.* |
| *present continuous* | fixed arrangement | *I'**m meeting** my tutor tomorrow.* |
| *present simple* | timetables, after *when / if / as soon as* | *The train **leaves** at 6. · When it **arrives**, …* |

### Common mistake
❌ *When the government will build the road, traffic will fall.* → ✅ *When the government **builds** the road, …*
