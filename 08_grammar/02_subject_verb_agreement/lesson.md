# Grammar — Subject–Verb Agreement

*Make verbs agree with their real subject, even in long noun phrases.*

## Core rule
The verb agrees with the **head noun** of the subject, not the noun closest to the verb.

- ✅ *The **number** of cars **has** increased.* (head = number, singular)
- ✅ *A large **number** of people **have** moved.* (a number of = many → plural)
- ✅ *The **percentage** of students who own laptops **is** high.*
- ✅ *One of the main **reasons** **is**…* (head = one)
- ✅ ***Each** of the countries **has**…*
- ✅ ***Everyone** **knows**…*, ***Nobody** **wants**…*

### Uncountable nouns are singular
*information, advice, research, evidence, equipment, traffic, news, knowledge* → **is / has / was**

### Watch out
- *The police **are**…* (plural)
- *People **are**…* (plural) — never *peoples are* when meaning persons.
- *Statistics **show**…* (plural when meaning figures)
