## Questions 1–8 · Multiple choice (one answer)

**1. B** — Head noun 'number' (with 'the') is singular.

**2. B** — 'A number of' = many → plural verb.

**3. A** — Head noun = 'one'.

**4. B** — 'Information' is uncountable → singular.

**5. B** — 'Each' is singular.

**6. A** — 'Research' is uncountable → singular.

**7. B** — 'Police' is always plural.

**8. B** — Head noun = 'proportion' (singular); 'who live alone' is a relative clause.
