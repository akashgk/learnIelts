# Vocabulary — Linking Words & Cohesion

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example | Collocations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **furthermore** | adv | adds a further point (formal) | *Furthermore, the policy would reduce costs.* | Furthermore, … |
| **however** | adv | introduces a contrast (new sentence) | *However, this view ignores the cost.* | However, … |
| **whereas** | conj | contrasts two facts in one sentence | *Men preferred football, whereas women preferred tennis.* | X, whereas Y |
| **despite** | prep | contrast + noun/-ing | *Despite the rain, the event continued.* | despite + noun / -ing |
| **although** | conj | contrast + clause | *Although it was raining, the event continued.* | although + clause |
| **consequently** | adv | introduces a result | *Fees rose. Consequently, applications fell.* | Consequently, … |
| **due to** | prep | because of + noun | *Flights were cancelled due to fog.* | due to + noun |
| **in contrast** | phrase | highlights a difference | *In contrast, rural areas saw a decline.* | In contrast, …; in contrast to + noun |
| **for instance** | phrase | introduces an example | *Some jobs, for instance nursing, are hard to automate.* | for instance / for example |
| **nevertheless** | adv | despite what was just said | *The plan is costly. Nevertheless, it is necessary.* | Nevertheless, … |
| **provided that** | conj | only if | *The scheme works provided that funding continues.* | provided (that) + clause |
| **in addition to** | prep | as well as + noun/-ing | *In addition to cost, there are safety concerns.* | in addition to + noun / -ing |

> **How to study:** cover the *Meaning* column and test yourself; then write your own sentence for each word using one of its collocations. Do the exercises in `QUESTIONS.md` and check `SOLUTION.md`.
