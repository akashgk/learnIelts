## Questions 1–6

*Complete each sentence with ONE word from the word list.*

## Questions 7–10

*Choose the correct letter, A, B or C.*

**7.** Which is grammatically correct?

- **A** Despite it was cold, we went out.
- **B** Despite the cold, we went out.
- **C** Despite of the cold, we went out.

**8.** Which linker introduces an example?

- **A** for instance
- **B** nevertheless
- **C** whereas

**9.** Which is correct?

- **A** However the plan failed.
- **B** However, the plan failed.
- **C** The plan, however failed.

**10.** 'Nevertheless' is closest in meaning to:

- **A** therefore
- **B** in spite of this
- **C** for example
