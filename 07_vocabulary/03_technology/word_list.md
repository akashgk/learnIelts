# Vocabulary — Technology & Innovation

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example | Collocations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **cutting-edge** | adj | the most advanced | *The lab uses cutting-edge equipment.* | cutting-edge technology / research |
| **obsolete** | adj | no longer used because something newer exists | *Fax machines are largely obsolete.* | become / render obsolete |
| **automation** | n | using machines instead of people | *Automation has replaced many factory jobs.* | increased automation |
| **breakthrough** | n | an important discovery | *Scientists made a major breakthrough in battery design.* | make / achieve a breakthrough |
| **innovation** | n | a new idea or method | *Innovation drives economic growth.* | encourage / foster innovation |
| **user-friendly** | adj | easy to use | *The app is very user-friendly.* | user-friendly design / interface |
| **privacy** | n | freedom from others knowing your personal information | *Social networks raise serious privacy concerns.* | invasion of privacy; privacy concerns |
| **screen time** | n | time spent using a device with a screen | *Doctors recommend limiting children's screen time.* | limit / reduce screen time |
| **digital divide** | n | gap between those with and without access to technology | *Rural schools are on the wrong side of the digital divide.* | bridge / close the digital divide |
| **artificial intelligence** | n | computer systems that perform tasks needing human intelligence | *Artificial intelligence is transforming medicine.* | advances in artificial intelligence |
| **addictive** | adj | making people want more and unable to stop | *Many apps are deliberately addictive.* | highly addictive |
| **connectivity** | n | being connected to the internet / networks | *Connectivity in remote areas has improved.* | internet / mobile connectivity |

> **How to study:** cover the *Meaning* column and test yourself; then write your own sentence for each word using one of its collocations. Do the exercises in `QUESTIONS.md` and check `SOLUTION.md`.
