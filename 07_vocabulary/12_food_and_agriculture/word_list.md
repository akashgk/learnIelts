# Vocabulary — Food & Agriculture

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example | Collocations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **staple** | n/adj | a basic, main food | *Rice is the staple food for half the world.* | staple food / crop / diet |
| **food security** | n | reliable access to enough affordable, nutritious food | *Droughts threaten food security in the region.* | ensure / threaten food security |
| **organic** | adj | produced without artificial chemicals | *Organic vegetables are often more expensive.* | organic farming / produce |
| **yield** | n/v | the amount produced by a crop | *New varieties have increased wheat yields.* | crop yields; high-yield |
| **intensive farming** | n | farming that maximises output from land | *Intensive farming can damage soil.* | intensive farming methods |
| **processed food** | n | food changed by industrial methods | *Processed food is often high in salt.* | highly processed food |
| **malnutrition** | n | poor health caused by a lack of proper food | *Malnutrition affects millions of children.* | suffer from malnutrition |
| **pesticide** | n | a chemical used to kill insects or pests | *Pesticide use is linked to declining bee numbers.* | pesticide use / residues |
| **livestock** | n | farm animals | *Livestock farming produces significant emissions.* | raise / rear livestock |
| **genetically modified** | adj | having altered DNA | *Genetically modified crops can resist drought.* | genetically modified (GM) crops |
| **self-sufficient** | adj | able to produce all you need | *The country is self-sufficient in rice.* | self-sufficient in food |
| **food miles** | n | the distance food travels from farm to consumer | *Buying local produce reduces food miles.* | reduce food miles |

> **How to study:** cover the *Meaning* column and test yourself; then write your own sentence for each word using one of its collocations. Do the exercises in `QUESTIONS.md` and check `SOLUTION.md`.
