# Vocabulary — Environment & Climate

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example | Collocations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **emissions** | n | gases released into the air, especially CO2 | *Aviation accounts for a growing share of carbon emissions.* | reduce / cut emissions; greenhouse gas emissions |
| **renewable** | adj | (energy) from sources that are not used up, e.g. wind, solar | *Renewable energy now supplies a fifth of the country's electricity.* | renewable energy / sources |
| **deforestation** | n | cutting down forests on a large scale | *Deforestation destroys habitats and releases stored carbon.* | tackle / halt deforestation |
| **biodiversity** | n | the variety of plant and animal life in an area | *Wetlands support remarkable biodiversity.* | loss of biodiversity; protect biodiversity |
| **sustainable** | adj | able to continue without damaging the environment | *We need more sustainable forms of transport.* | sustainable development / practices |
| **mitigate** | v | to make something bad less severe | *Planting trees can help mitigate the effects of urban heat.* | mitigate the impact / effects / risk |
| **degradation** | n | the process of becoming worse in quality | *Overgrazing has caused severe land degradation.* | environmental / land degradation |
| **fossil fuels** | n | coal, oil and gas | *Many countries still depend heavily on fossil fuels.* | burn / phase out fossil fuels |
| **conservation** | n | protection of nature and wildlife | *Tourist fees help fund conservation projects.* | wildlife / marine conservation |
| **pollutant** | n | a substance that pollutes | *Ozone is a pollutant that aggravates asthma.* | air / harmful pollutants |
| **carbon footprint** | n | the amount of CO2 produced by a person or activity | *Eating less meat can reduce your carbon footprint.* | reduce / offset your carbon footprint |
| **endangered** | adj | at risk of extinction | *The tiger remains an endangered species.* | endangered species; critically endangered |

> **How to study:** cover the *Meaning* column and test yourself; then write your own sentence for each word using one of its collocations. Do the exercises in `QUESTIONS.md` and check `SOLUTION.md`.
