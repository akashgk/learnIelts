# Vocabulary — Education & Learning

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example | Collocations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **curriculum** | n | the subjects studied in a school or course | *Coding has been added to the national curriculum.* | broad / national curriculum |
| **tuition fees** | n | money paid for teaching at a university/college | *Tuition fees have tripled in a decade.* | pay / abolish tuition fees |
| **rote learning** | n | learning by repetition without understanding | *Critics say the system relies too much on rote learning.* | rely on rote learning |
| **critical thinking** | n | analysing information objectively before judging | *Debates help develop critical thinking.* | develop / encourage critical thinking |
| **vocational** | adj | related to skills for a specific job | *Vocational courses train students as electricians or chefs.* | vocational training / qualifications |
| **literacy** | n | the ability to read and write | *Adult literacy rates have risen sharply.* | literacy rate; digital literacy |
| **academic performance** | n | how well a student does in study | *Sleep strongly affects academic performance.* | improve academic performance |
| **extracurricular** | adj | outside the normal curriculum | *Extracurricular activities include sport and drama clubs.* | extracurricular activities |
| **tertiary education** | n | university / college level | *More women than men now enter tertiary education.* | access to tertiary education |
| **acquire** | v | to gain knowledge or skills | *Children acquire languages quickly.* | acquire skills / knowledge |
| **assessment** | n | judging a student's work/ability | *Continuous assessment reduces exam stress.* | continuous / formal assessment |
| **drop out** | phr v | leave school/course before finishing | *Financial pressure causes many students to drop out.* | drop out of school / university |

> **How to study:** cover the *Meaning* column and test yourself; then write your own sentence for each word using one of its collocations. Do the exercises in `QUESTIONS.md` and check `SOLUTION.md`.
