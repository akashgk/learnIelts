# Vocabulary — Media & Advertising

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example | Collocations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **mainstream media** | n | large, established news organisations | *Many young people distrust the mainstream media.* | coverage in the mainstream media |
| **misinformation** | n | false information spread, often unintentionally | *Misinformation spreads quickly online.* | spread misinformation |
| **influencer** | n | a person who affects followers' choices online | *Brands pay influencers to promote products.* | social media influencer |
| **target audience** | n | the group a product/message is aimed at | *The target audience is teenagers.* | reach a target audience |
| **consumerism** | n | focus on buying goods | *Advertising fuels consumerism.* | rampant consumerism |
| **persuasive** | adj | able to convince | *The advert uses persuasive language.* | persuasive techniques |
| **coverage** | n | reporting of news | *The event received wide coverage.* | media / extensive coverage |
| **sensationalism** | n | exaggerating stories to shock | *Tabloids are criticised for sensationalism.* | accused of sensationalism |
| **bias** | n | unfair preference for one side | *The report showed clear political bias.* | political / media bias |
| **censor** | v | remove parts considered unacceptable | *Some scenes were censored.* | heavily censored |
| **endorse** | v | publicly support a product | *Athletes endorse sports brands.* | celebrity endorsement |
| **gullible** | adj | easily tricked | *Children can be gullible about adverts.* | gullible consumers |

> **How to study:** cover the *Meaning* column and test yourself; then write your own sentence for each word using one of its collocations. Do the exercises in `QUESTIONS.md` and check `SOLUTION.md`.
