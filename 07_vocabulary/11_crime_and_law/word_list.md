# Vocabulary — Crime & Law

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example | Collocations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **offender** | n | a person who has committed a crime | *Young offenders need support as well as punishment.* | young / repeat offender |
| **deter** | v | to discourage someone from doing something | *Visible policing can deter burglars.* | deter crime / criminals |
| **custodial sentence** | n | a punishment of time in prison | *Short custodial sentences rarely reduce reoffending.* | receive / impose a custodial sentence |
| **reoffend** | v | to commit another crime after punishment | *Education in prison reduces the likelihood that people reoffend.* | rate of reoffending |
| **community service** | n | unpaid work as a punishment | *He was sentenced to 200 hours of community service.* | do / be sentenced to community service |
| **law enforcement** | n | the police and other agencies that apply the law | *Law enforcement agencies are using facial recognition.* | law enforcement agencies |
| **petty crime** | n | minor crime such as shoplifting | *Petty crime has risen in the city centre.* | petty crime / theft |
| **cybercrime** | n | crime committed using computers or the internet | *Cybercrime costs businesses billions each year.* | tackle cybercrime |
| **legislation** | n | laws, or the process of making them | *Stricter legislation on knives was introduced.* | introduce / enforce legislation |
| **rehabilitate** | v | to help an offender return to normal life | *Prisons should rehabilitate rather than merely punish.* | rehabilitate offenders |
| **surveillance** | n | careful watching, often by police or cameras | *CCTV surveillance is common in city centres.* | CCTV / mass surveillance |
| **juvenile** | adj | relating to young people under 18 | *Juvenile crime is often linked to poverty.* | juvenile crime / court |

> **How to study:** cover the *Meaning* column and test yourself; then write your own sentence for each word using one of its collocations. Do the exercises in `QUESTIONS.md` and check `SOLUTION.md`.
