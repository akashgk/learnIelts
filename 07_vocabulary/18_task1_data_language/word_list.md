# Vocabulary — Task 1 Data Language

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example | Collocations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **surge** | v/n | a sudden large increase | *Sales surged in December.* | surge to; a surge in |
| **plummet** | v | to fall very quickly | *Profits plummeted during the crisis.* | plummet to |
| **plateau** | v/n | to stay level after rising | *Numbers plateaued at around 500.* | reach a plateau |
| **fluctuate** | v | to rise and fall irregularly | *Prices fluctuated between $10 and $15.* | fluctuate between … and … |
| **peak** | v/n | to reach the highest point | *Visitors peaked at 2 million in 2019.* | peak at; reach a peak of |
| **marginal** | adj | very small | *There was a marginal increase.* | marginal increase / decline |
| **proportion** | n | part of a whole, often % | *The proportion of women rose.* | the proportion of |
| **respectively** | adv | in the order mentioned | *A and B grew by 5% and 8% respectively.* | X and Y … respectively |
| **account for** | phr v | to form a part of a total | *Rent accounts for 40% of spending.* | account for X% |
| **overtake** | v | to become higher than | *Gas overtook coal in 2015.* | overtake X as |
| **trough** | n | the lowest point | *Sales hit a trough in March.* | hit / reach a trough |
| **twofold** | adj/adv | double | *There was a twofold increase.* | a twofold / threefold increase |

> **How to study:** cover the *Meaning* column and test yourself; then write your own sentence for each word using one of its collocations. Do the exercises in `QUESTIONS.md` and check `SOLUTION.md`.
