# Vocabulary — Arts & Culture

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example | Collocations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **heritage** | n | traditions, buildings and objects from the past | *The festival celebrates the region's cultural heritage.* | cultural / national heritage |
| **exhibition** | n | a public display of art or objects | *The museum is hosting an exhibition of Indian textiles.* | hold / host an exhibition |
| **performing arts** | n | music, dance and drama | *Funding for the performing arts has been cut.* | study the performing arts |
| **creativity** | n | the ability to produce original ideas | *Art lessons foster creativity.* | foster / stifle creativity |
| **aesthetic** | adj | relating to beauty | *The building has great aesthetic appeal.* | aesthetic value / appeal |
| **contemporary** | adj | modern; belonging to the present | *She collects contemporary art.* | contemporary art / music |
| **masterpiece** | n | an outstanding work of art | *The painting is considered a masterpiece.* | a literary masterpiece |
| **patron** | n | a person who supports the arts with money | *Wealthy patrons funded Renaissance artists.* | patron of the arts |
| **subsidy** | n | money given by government to reduce costs | *Theatres rely on state subsidies.* | government / state subsidy |
| **preserve** | v | to keep something in its original state | *We must preserve traditional crafts.* | preserve traditions / heritage |
| **mainstream** | adj/n | accepted by most people | *Hip-hop has become mainstream.* | mainstream culture / media |
| **niche** | adj/n | appealing to a small, specialised group | *Jazz now has a niche audience.* | niche market / audience |

> **How to study:** cover the *Meaning* column and test yourself; then write your own sentence for each word using one of its collocations. Do the exercises in `QUESTIONS.md` and check `SOLUTION.md`.
