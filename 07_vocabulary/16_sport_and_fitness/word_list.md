# Vocabulary — Sport & Fitness

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example | Collocations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **sedentary** | adj | involving little physical activity | *Sedentary jobs increase the risk of back pain.* | sedentary lifestyle |
| **work out** | phr v | to exercise, especially in a gym | *I work out three times a week.* | work out at the gym |
| **stamina** | n | the ability to keep going for a long time | *Long-distance running builds stamina.* | build / improve stamina |
| **spectator** | n | a person watching an event | *The stadium holds 50,000 spectators.* | spectator sport |
| **amateur** | adj/n | doing something without being paid | *He plays for an amateur football club.* | amateur athlete |
| **sponsorship** | n | financial support from a company | *Sponsorship deals are worth millions.* | sponsorship deal |
| **performance-enhancing drugs** | n | illegal substances that improve performance | *Athletes caught using performance-enhancing drugs are banned.* | take / test for performance-enhancing drugs |
| **grassroots** | adj | at the basic, local level | *Funding for grassroots sport is vital.* | grassroots sport / level |
| **competitive** | adj | involving competition; wanting to win | *She is very competitive.* | competitive sport; highly competitive |
| **fitness** | n | the condition of being physically fit | *Swimming improves overall fitness.* | physical fitness; fitness levels |
| **injury** | n | physical harm | *He missed the season through injury.* | suffer / sustain an injury |
| **host** | v | to organise and hold an event | *The city will host the Olympics.* | host the Olympics / World Cup |

> **How to study:** cover the *Meaning* column and test yourself; then write your own sentence for each word using one of its collocations. Do the exercises in `QUESTIONS.md` and check `SOLUTION.md`.
