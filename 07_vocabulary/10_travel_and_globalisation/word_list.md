# Vocabulary — Travel & Globalisation

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example | Collocations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **globalisation** | n | the increasing connection of the world's economies and cultures | *Globalisation has lowered prices of goods.* | effects of globalisation |
| **multicultural** | adj | including many cultures | *London is a multicultural city.* | multicultural society |
| **mass tourism** | n | tourism by very large numbers of people | *Mass tourism damages fragile sites.* | impact of mass tourism |
| **eco-tourism** | n | responsible travel to natural areas | *Eco-tourism funds rainforest protection.* | promote eco-tourism |
| **homogenisation** | n | becoming the same | *Critics fear the homogenisation of culture.* | cultural homogenisation |
| **heritage** | n | traditions and buildings from the past | *The old town is a World Heritage Site.* | cultural heritage; heritage site |
| **broaden horizons** | phrase | to experience new things and ideas | *Travel broadens your horizons.* | broaden your horizons |
| **culture shock** | n | confusion when living in a new culture | *Many students experience culture shock.* | suffer / experience culture shock |
| **expatriate** | n | a person living outside their home country | *Dubai has a large expatriate community.* | expatriate workers |
| **off the beaten track** | phrase | away from popular places | *We stayed in a village off the beaten track.* | go off the beaten track |
| **multinational** | adj/n | operating in many countries | *Multinational companies dominate the market.* | multinational corporation |
| **influx** | n | a large arrival of people/things | *The island faces an influx of tourists every summer.* | an influx of visitors / migrants |

> **How to study:** cover the *Meaning* column and test yourself; then write your own sentence for each word using one of its collocations. Do the exercises in `QUESTIONS.md` and check `SOLUTION.md`.
