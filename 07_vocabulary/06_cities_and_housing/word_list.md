# Vocabulary — Cities & Housing

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example | Collocations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **urbanisation** | n | the growth of cities as people move from the countryside | *Rapid urbanisation strains infrastructure.* | rapid urbanisation |
| **infrastructure** | n | basic systems: roads, water, power | *The city must invest in infrastructure.* | transport / public infrastructure |
| **congestion** | n | too much traffic | *Congestion costs the economy billions.* | traffic congestion; congestion charge |
| **affordable housing** | n | homes that people on average incomes can afford | *There is a shortage of affordable housing.* | build / provide affordable housing |
| **urban sprawl** | n | uncontrolled spread of a city into countryside | *Urban sprawl increases car dependency.* | limit urban sprawl |
| **amenities** | n | useful facilities: shops, parks, sports centres | *The area lacks basic amenities.* | local amenities |
| **overcrowding** | n | too many people in a space | *Overcrowding in flats spreads disease.* | severe overcrowding |
| **regenerate** | v | to improve a run-down area | *The old docks have been regenerated.* | regenerate an area |
| **pedestrianise** | v | close a street to vehicles | *The high street was pedestrianised in 2010.* | pedestrianised zone |
| **commute** | v/n | to travel regularly to work | *Many people commute over an hour each way.* | daily commute |
| **high-rise** | adj/n | a tall building with many floors | *High-rise flats save land.* | high-rise apartments |
| **suburbs** | n | residential areas on the edge of a city | *Families often move to the suburbs.* | live in the suburbs |

> **How to study:** cover the *Meaning* column and test yourself; then write your own sentence for each word using one of its collocations. Do the exercises in `QUESTIONS.md` and check `SOLUTION.md`.
