# Vocabulary — Government & Society

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example | Collocations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **legislation** | n | laws | *New legislation bans single-use plastics.* | introduce / pass legislation |
| **taxpayer** | n | a person who pays tax | *The project was funded by the taxpayer.* | at the taxpayer's expense |
| **welfare** | n | state help for people in need | *Welfare spending has increased.* | welfare state / benefits |
| **inequality** | n | unfair difference between groups | *Income inequality is growing.* | social / income inequality |
| **subsidise** | v | to pay part of the cost | *The government subsidises public transport.* | heavily subsidised |
| **regulate** | v | control with rules | *Governments must regulate social media.* | strictly regulated |
| **public services** | n | services provided by the state | *Taxes fund public services like healthcare.* | invest in public services |
| **deterrent** | n | something that discourages action | *Fines act as a deterrent to speeding.* | act as / serve as a deterrent |
| **rehabilitation** | n | helping offenders return to normal life | *Prisons should focus on rehabilitation.* | rehabilitation programmes |
| **censorship** | n | controlling what can be published | *Internet censorship limits free speech.* | state censorship |
| **social mobility** | n | ability to move to a higher social class | *Free education promotes social mobility.* | improve social mobility |
| **allocate** | v | give resources for a purpose | *More funding should be allocated to schools.* | allocate funds / resources |

> **How to study:** cover the *Meaning* column and test yourself; then write your own sentence for each word using one of its collocations. Do the exercises in `QUESTIONS.md` and check `SOLUTION.md`.
