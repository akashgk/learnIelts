# Vocabulary — Work & Economy

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example | Collocations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **unemployment** | n | not having a job | *Youth unemployment remains high.* | unemployment rate; youth unemployment |
| **job security** | n | confidence that you will keep your job | *Freelancers have little job security.* | lack of job security |
| **work–life balance** | n | balance between work and personal life | *Flexible hours improve work–life balance.* | achieve / maintain a healthy work–life balance |
| **remuneration** | n | payment for work (formal) | *Remuneration includes salary and bonuses.* | generous remuneration |
| **productivity** | n | output per worker / hour | *Short breaks can boost productivity.* | boost / increase productivity |
| **gig economy** | n | short-term, freelance work, often via apps | *Delivery riders are part of the gig economy.* | work in the gig economy |
| **recession** | n | a period when the economy shrinks | *Thousands lost jobs during the recession.* | go into / enter a recession |
| **entrepreneur** | n | someone who starts a business | *Young entrepreneurs need access to loans.* | aspiring entrepreneur |
| **inflation** | n | general rise in prices | *Inflation reduces the value of savings.* | high / rising inflation |
| **workforce** | n | all the workers in a company/country | *Women make up half the workforce.* | skilled workforce |
| **promotion** | n | a move to a higher job | *She was given a promotion after a year.* | get / be given a promotion |
| **outsource** | v | to pay another company to do work | *Many firms outsource customer service.* | outsource jobs / production |

> **How to study:** cover the *Meaning* column and test yourself; then write your own sentence for each word using one of its collocations. Do the exercises in `QUESTIONS.md` and check `SOLUTION.md`.
