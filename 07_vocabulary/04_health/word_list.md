# Vocabulary — Health & Lifestyle

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example | Collocations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **sedentary** | adj | involving a lot of sitting, little exercise | *Office workers often have sedentary lifestyles.* | sedentary lifestyle / job |
| **obesity** | n | being very overweight | *Childhood obesity has doubled in thirty years.* | childhood obesity; obesity rates |
| **life expectancy** | n | how long people are expected to live | *Life expectancy has risen to over 80.* | average life expectancy |
| **well-being** | n | being healthy and happy | *Green spaces improve mental well-being.* | mental / physical well-being |
| **preventable** | adj | can be stopped from happening | *Many deaths from heart disease are preventable.* | preventable disease |
| **balanced diet** | n | food with the right mix of nutrients | *A balanced diet includes fruit and vegetables.* | eat / maintain a balanced diet |
| **chronic** | adj | lasting a long time (illness) | *Diabetes is a chronic condition.* | chronic disease / pain |
| **vaccination** | n | giving a vaccine to protect against disease | *Vaccination has eliminated smallpox.* | vaccination programme / rates |
| **healthcare** | n | medical services | *Universal healthcare is funded by taxes.* | access to healthcare; healthcare system |
| **burnout** | n | exhaustion from long-term stress | *Long hours lead to burnout among nurses.* | suffer from burnout |
| **epidemic** | n | a disease spreading widely in a community | *Health officials warn of an obesity epidemic.* | outbreak of an epidemic |
| **detrimental** | adj | harmful | *Lack of sleep is detrimental to health.* | detrimental effect on |

> **How to study:** cover the *Meaning* column and test yourself; then write your own sentence for each word using one of its collocations. Do the exercises in `QUESTIONS.md` and check `SOLUTION.md`.
