# Vocabulary — Language & Communication

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example | Collocations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **fluent** | adj | able to speak smoothly and accurately | *She is fluent in three languages.* | fluent in; fluent English |
| **native speaker** | n | someone who learned a language from birth | *Native speakers rarely notice their own grammar.* | native speaker of English |
| **bilingual** | adj | able to use two languages | *Bilingual children switch between languages easily.* | bilingual education |
| **dialect** | n | a regional form of a language | *The village has its own dialect.* | regional dialect |
| **lingua franca** | n | a shared language between speakers of different languages | *English is the lingua franca of aviation.* | global lingua franca |
| **articulate** | adj/v | able to express ideas clearly | *She is an articulate speaker.* | articulate speaker; articulate ideas |
| **body language** | n | communication through gestures and posture | *Body language can reveal nervousness.* | read someone's body language |
| **misunderstanding** | n | failure to understand correctly | *Cultural differences can cause misunderstandings.* | lead to a misunderstanding |
| **acquire** | v | to learn or gain | *Children acquire language naturally.* | acquire a language |
| **proficiency** | n | a high level of skill | *The job requires proficiency in Spanish.* | language proficiency |
| **endangered language** | n | a language at risk of disappearing | *Many endangered languages have few speakers.* | revive an endangered language |
| **convey** | v | to communicate an idea or feeling | *Tone of voice conveys emotion.* | convey meaning / a message |

> **How to study:** cover the *Meaning* column and test yourself; then write your own sentence for each word using one of its collocations. Do the exercises in `QUESTIONS.md` and check `SOLUTION.md`.
