# Vocabulary — Science & Research

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example | Collocations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **hypothesis** | n | an idea to be tested | *The experiment tested the hypothesis that sleep improves memory.* | test / support a hypothesis |
| **empirical** | adj | based on observation or experiment | *There is little empirical evidence for the claim.* | empirical evidence / data |
| **conduct** | v | to carry out (research) | *Researchers conducted a study of 500 patients.* | conduct research / an experiment |
| **findings** | n | results of research | *The findings were published last year.* | research findings |
| **peer review** | n | evaluation by other experts before publication | *All papers go through peer review.* | peer-reviewed journal |
| **replicate** | v | repeat an experiment and get the same result | *Other teams failed to replicate the results.* | replicate a study / findings |
| **sample** | n | a group chosen for study | *The sample was too small to be reliable.* | representative / large sample |
| **variable** | n | a factor that can change | *Age is an important variable.* | control for variables |
| **correlation** | n | a connection between two things | *There is a correlation between income and health.* | strong correlation; correlation does not imply causation |
| **phenomenon** | n (pl. phenomena) | something that exists or happens, often unusual | *Bioluminescence is a fascinating phenomenon.* | natural phenomenon |
| **breakthrough** | n | important discovery | *A breakthrough in gene editing.* | scientific breakthrough |
| **funding** | n | money for a purpose | *Research depends on government funding.* | secure / cut funding |

> **How to study:** cover the *Meaning* column and test yourself; then write your own sentence for each word using one of its collocations. Do the exercises in `QUESTIONS.md` and check `SOLUTION.md`.
