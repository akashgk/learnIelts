# Writing Task 2 — Discussion (both views + your opinion)

*You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.*

> **Some people think that governments should spend money on supporting the arts, such as music, theatre and museums. Others believe this money would be better spent on public services such as healthcare and education. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.**

*Write at least 250 words.*

---

### How to approach this question type
- Avoid a false choice: you can argue for a **balance**, but your opinion must still be clear.
- Give concrete examples of what arts funding achieves (tourism, jobs, access for poorer families, national identity).
