# Writing Task 2 — Opinion (agree / disagree)

*You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.*

> **Wealthy nations should be required to share their wealth with poorer nations by providing things such as food and education. To what extent do you agree or disagree?**

*Write at least 250 words.*

---

### Tip
Note the words **"required"** and **"such as food and education"**. You can agree with helping poorer countries while questioning *how* aid should be given.
