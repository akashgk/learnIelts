# Model essay (Band 8–9)

The enormous gap in living standards between rich and poor countries has led some people to argue that wealthy nations have an obligation to share their resources. I agree that richer countries have a responsibility to help, but I believe the form this assistance takes is just as important as the obligation itself.

There are strong moral and practical reasons for wealthy nations to provide aid. Morally, it seems unjustifiable that some populations enjoy abundance while others suffer from hunger and preventable disease, particularly when much of the wealth of developed countries was built, at least in part, through trade and colonial relationships that disadvantaged poorer regions. Practically, global poverty affects everyone. Instability, mass migration and the spread of infectious diseases do not respect national borders, so investing in the development of poorer countries is also in the long-term interest of richer ones.

However, simply sending food and other goods is not always the most effective solution. Emergency food aid is essential during famines and natural disasters, but if it is provided over long periods, it can undercut local farmers and create dependency. Education, by contrast, offers more lasting benefits, as it equips people with the skills to improve their own lives and build their countries' economies. Other forms of support, such as fair trade agreements, debt relief and the sharing of medical and agricultural technology, may also achieve more than direct handouts.

Furthermore, aid is most successful when it is designed in partnership with recipient countries rather than imposed from outside, and when safeguards are in place to prevent money from being lost to corruption.

In conclusion, I agree that wealthy countries should be expected to help poorer nations, but this help should focus on education and sustainable development rather than on long-term food donations.

*(296 words)*

## Key moves
- **Agree with a qualification:** *I agree that… but I believe the form this assistance takes is just as important.*
- Body 1 uses **two types of reason** (moral + practical/self-interest) — a quick way to develop ideas.
- **Vocabulary:** *obligation, unjustifiable, abundance, preventable disease, undercut, dependency, debt relief, fair trade, recipient countries, safeguards, corruption, sustainable development*.
