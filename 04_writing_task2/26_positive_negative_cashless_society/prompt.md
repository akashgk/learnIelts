# Writing Task 2 — Positive or negative development

*You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.*

> **In many countries, people are using cash less and less, and paying for most things by card or mobile phone. Is this a positive or negative development?**

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

*Write at least 250 words.*

---

### How to approach it
- A balanced but decided answer works well: mostly positive, *provided* certain risks are managed.
