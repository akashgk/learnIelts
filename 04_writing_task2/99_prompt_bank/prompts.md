# Writing Task 2 — Prompt bank

Extra practice prompts **without** model answers. Pick one, plan for 5 minutes, write for 35, then self-assess with the checklist below and compare your structure with the model essay of the same type in this section.

## Opinion
*Question wording: To what extent do you agree or disagree?*

1. **[education]** Schools should teach practical skills such as cooking and managing money instead of academic subjects like history and literature. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
2. **[education]** All students should be required to study a foreign language until the end of secondary school. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
3. **[education]** Homework is unnecessary and should be abolished in primary schools. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
4. **[technology]** Children under the age of 13 should not be allowed to own a smartphone. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
5. **[technology]** Social media companies should be held legally responsible for false information posted on their platforms. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
6. **[work]** A four-day working week would benefit both employees and employers. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
7. **[work]** Companies should be required to pay men and women the same salary for the same job, and publish this information. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
8. **[environment]** Governments should impose heavy taxes on flights to reduce their environmental impact. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
9. **[environment]** Single-use plastic products should be completely banned. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
10. **[health]** Junk food advertising should be banned on television and online. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
11. **[health]** The government, not individuals, is responsible for public health. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
12. **[society]** Older people should be encouraged to continue working beyond the retirement age. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
13. **[cities]** New housing developments should always include green spaces, even if this means fewer homes are built. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
14. **[crime]** Prison sentences are the best way to reduce crime. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
15. **[culture]** Museums and art galleries should be free for everyone. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
16. **[media]** News reporting today focuses too much on negative stories. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
17. **[sport]** Professional athletes are paid far too much money. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
18. **[globalisation]** International English tests should be replaced by assessments of practical communication skills. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

## Discussion
*Question wording: Discuss both views and give your own opinion.*

19. **[education]** Some people think universities should provide graduates with the knowledge and skills needed in the workplace. Others think the true function of a university is to give access to knowledge for its own sake. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
20. **[education]** Some believe children should start formal education at a very early age, while others think they should not begin school until they are at least seven. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
21. **[technology]** Some people think technology has made our lives more complicated, while others believe it has made life easier. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
22. **[work]** Some people prefer to work for a large company, while others prefer a small business or self-employment. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
23. **[environment]** Some say that protecting the environment is the responsibility of governments, while others say individuals should do more. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
24. **[society]** Some people think that young people should be free to choose their own career, while others think parents should help decide. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
25. **[cities]** Some believe that living in a big city is good for people, while others think it is harmful. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
26. **[crime]** Some people think the best way to reduce crime is longer prison sentences, while others believe education is more effective. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
27. **[culture]** Some people believe that traditional cultures will disappear because of globalisation, while others think they will survive. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
28. **[health]** Some think that sport at school should be compulsory, while others feel students should choose whether to take part. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
29. **[media]** Some people say advertising encourages us to buy things we do not need. Others say it provides useful information about new products. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
30. **[government]** Some argue that governments should spend money on railways, while others think it should be spent on roads. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

## Advantages Disadvantages
*Question wording: What are the advantages and disadvantages? / Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?*

31. **[education]** More students are choosing to take a gap year between school and university. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?
32. **[work]** Many people now work in the 'gig economy', doing short-term freelance jobs. What are the advantages and disadvantages?
33. **[technology]** Artificial intelligence is increasingly used to make decisions in areas such as hiring and lending. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?
34. **[globalisation]** Many students now study in a foreign country. What are the advantages and disadvantages for the students and for the host country?
35. **[cities]** In some countries, more people are choosing to live in apartments rather than houses. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?
36. **[society]** More and more people are using cashless payment methods. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?
37. **[tourism]** Some countries are developing space tourism. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?
38. **[food]** Many people now buy ready-made meals instead of cooking. What are the advantages and disadvantages?

## Problem Solution
*Question wording: What are the causes/problems? What solutions can you suggest?*

39. **[health]** Obesity rates among children are rising in many countries. What are the causes, and what solutions can you suggest?
40. **[environment]** Many species of plants and animals are becoming extinct. Why is this happening, and what can be done to prevent it?
41. **[cities]** Housing in many large cities has become unaffordable for ordinary workers. What problems does this cause, and how can it be solved?
42. **[society]** Many people feel lonely, even though they live in busy cities. What are the causes, and what can be done?
43. **[education]** Many students find it difficult to concentrate in class. What are the reasons, and how can this be addressed?
44. **[technology]** Online fraud and scams are increasing. What are the causes, and what measures could reduce them?
45. **[work]** Many employees suffer from stress at work. What are the causes, and what can employers do about it?
46. **[environment]** Food waste is a major problem in developed countries. Why does it happen, and what can be done?
47. **[crime]** Crime rates among teenagers have increased in some areas. What are the causes, and what are the solutions?

## Two Part
*Question wording: Answer both questions.*

48. **[society]** Many people today do not know their neighbours. Why is this? What can be done to build stronger communities?
49. **[technology]** People today spend more time looking at screens than ever before. Why is this? Is it a positive or negative development?
50. **[work]** Many people change careers several times during their lives. Why do they do this? Is it a positive trend?
51. **[culture]** Fewer people are learning traditional skills such as handicrafts. Why is this happening? Should these skills be preserved?
52. **[health]** Many people choose to follow a vegetarian or vegan diet. What are the reasons? Should more people do the same?
53. **[education]** In many countries, fewer students are studying science subjects at university. Why is this? What can be done to encourage them?
54. **[media]** Many people now get their news from social media rather than newspapers. Why is this? What problems might it cause?

## Positive Negative
*Question wording: Is this a positive or negative development?*

55. **[society]** In many countries, people are getting married and having children later in life. Is this a positive or negative development?
56. **[globalisation]** English is becoming the main language of international communication. Is this a positive or negative development?
57. **[technology]** More and more businesses are using robots to replace human workers. Is this a positive or negative development?
58. **[cities]** Many shopping malls are being built in cities while local markets are disappearing. Is this a positive or negative development?
59. **[education]** Many universities now offer degree courses entirely online. Is this a positive or negative development?
60. **[tourism]** Tourists increasingly visit remote, natural places that were previously untouched. Is this a positive or negative development?

## Self-assessment checklist
- [ ] Did I answer **every part** of the question?
- [ ] Is my **position** clear in the introduction and conclusion (if one is asked for)?
- [ ] Does each body paragraph have **one main idea**, explained and supported with an example?
- [ ] Did I use a **range of linkers** without overusing them?
- [ ] Did I use **topic vocabulary** accurately (see `07_vocabulary`)?
- [ ] Did I check **articles, plurals, subject–verb agreement**?
- [ ] Is it **250+ words**?
