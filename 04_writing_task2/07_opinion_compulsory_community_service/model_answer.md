# Model essay (Band 8–9)

It has been suggested that secondary school students should be obliged to undertake unpaid work in their local community as part of their education. I strongly agree with this proposal, as I believe the benefits for both young people and society outweigh the concerns about making such work mandatory.

The most important reason for compulsory community service is that it develops qualities that academic study alone cannot. When teenagers coach younger children or help at a food bank, they learn to take responsibility, communicate with people of different ages and backgrounds, and work as part of a team. These are precisely the skills that employers and universities value, and many students discover interests or career paths they would never otherwise have considered.

Making the scheme compulsory, rather than voluntary, is also essential for fairness. Under voluntary systems, it is typically students from more privileged families — whose parents have the time, connections and awareness of its value — who take part. A requirement ensures that every student, regardless of background, gains this experience. Furthermore, communities themselves gain from thousands of hours of practical help, from cleaning parks to supporting elderly residents.

Critics argue that forcing students to volunteer contradicts the spirit of volunteering and adds to the pressure on teenagers who already have heavy workloads. These concerns are understandable, but they can be addressed through sensible design. If students are allowed to choose from a wide range of activities and the time commitment is modest — perhaps two hours a week — the experience is far more likely to be meaningful than burdensome.

In conclusion, I firmly support making community service a required part of high school education, since it builds valuable life skills, promotes equality of opportunity and strengthens local communities.

*(288 words)*

## Essay plan

| Paragraph | Content |
|---|---|
| Intro | Paraphrase + **strongly agree** |
| Body 1 | Reason 1: develops skills (responsibility, communication, teamwork, career ideas) |
| Body 2 | Reason 2: **why compulsory** — fairness; community benefits |
| Body 3 | Counter-argument + rebuttal (pressure / spirit of volunteering → choice + small time commitment) |
| Conclusion | Restate |

## Why the rebuttal paragraph matters
Acknowledging and **answering** the opposing view (*Critics argue… These concerns are understandable, but…*) shows a fully developed position — a Band 8+ feature of Task Response.
