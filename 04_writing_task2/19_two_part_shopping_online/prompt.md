# Writing Task 2 — Two-part question

*You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.*

> **More and more people are shopping online rather than in shops. Why is this happening? What effects does this have on town and city centres?**

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

*Write at least 250 words.*

---

### How to approach it
- Answer Q1 (reasons) and Q2 (effects) in separate paragraphs.
- Effects can be both negative and positive — showing both is a sign of depth.
