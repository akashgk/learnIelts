# Model essay (Band 8–9)

Cheaper flights and rising incomes have made international travel accessible to far more people than ever before. Although mass tourism has created real problems, I believe its advantages outweigh its disadvantages, provided it is properly managed.

The drawbacks of large-scale tourism are clearly visible in many popular destinations. Historic cities such as Venice and Barcelona have become so crowded at peak times that local residents complain of being driven out, as homes are converted into short-term holiday rentals and rents rise beyond their reach. Tourism also carries an environmental cost: aviation is a significant source of carbon emissions, and fragile ecosystems such as coral reefs can be damaged by careless visitors. In some cases, local traditions are reduced to commercial performances designed to entertain tourists.

Nevertheless, these disadvantages are outweighed by the substantial benefits tourism brings. Economically, it is one of the world's largest employers, providing jobs in hotels, restaurants, transport and crafts, often in regions with few other industries. For many developing countries, tourist spending is a vital source of foreign currency that funds infrastructure, schools and hospitals. Tourism can also support conservation, as entrance fees to national parks and heritage sites pay for their protection. Perhaps most importantly, travel promotes understanding between cultures. Meeting people from different backgrounds can challenge stereotypes and foster tolerance in an increasingly interconnected world.

Moreover, many of the problems associated with tourism can be reduced through sensible policies, such as limiting holiday rentals, charging tourist taxes and controlling visitor numbers at sensitive sites.

In conclusion, while international tourism can harm communities and the environment, its economic, cultural and conservation benefits are greater, and its negative effects can largely be controlled through effective regulation.

*(280 words)*

---

## Key moves
- **Weaker side first, stronger side second** — the essay builds towards its conclusion.
- **"Nevertheless, these disadvantages are outweighed by…"** — directly answers the question at the start of Body 2.
- **Vocabulary:** *mass tourism, peak times, short-term rentals, fragile ecosystems, foreign currency, conservation, heritage sites, foster tolerance, tourist taxes, regulation*.
