# Writing Task 2 — Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?

*You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.*

> **International tourism has grown enormously in recent decades. Do the advantages of this development outweigh the disadvantages?**

*Write at least 250 words.*

---

### How to approach this question type
- Unlike essay 03, this question **requires a judgement**: advantages outweigh / disadvantages outweigh.
- Put the side you believe is weaker first, then the stronger side, so the essay builds towards your conclusion.
