# Writing Task 2 — Opinion (agree / disagree)

*You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.*

> **Some people believe that university education should be free for all students, regardless of their background. To what extent do you agree or disagree?**

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

*Write at least 250 words.*

---

### How to approach this question type
- **"To what extent do you agree or disagree?"** → you must give a **clear position** in the introduction and keep it consistent to the end.
- You may agree fully, disagree fully, or **partly agree** — a partial position is fine if it is clear (e.g. "free for those who cannot afford it, but not for everyone").
- Each body paragraph = **one main reason** + explanation + example.
