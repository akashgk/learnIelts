# Writing Task 2 — Two-part (direct) question

*You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.*

> **In many countries, people are moving from rural areas to cities. Why is this happening? Is this a positive or negative development?**

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

*Write at least 250 words.*

---

### How to approach this question type
- There are **two separate questions**. Answer each one fully, ideally in its own body paragraph.
- The second question asks for your **judgement** — make it clear (positive, negative, or mixed with a final verdict).
- Don't spend 80% of the essay on the first question; balance matters.
