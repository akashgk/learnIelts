# Writing Task 2 — Advantages and disadvantages

*You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.*

> **An increasing number of people now work from home rather than in an office. What are the advantages and disadvantages of this development?**

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

*Write at least 250 words.*

---

### How to approach this question type
- This version does **not** ask for your opinion — you can stay neutral. (If the question says *"Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?"* you **must** give an opinion — see essay 10.)
- Body 1 = advantages (2 ideas), Body 2 = disadvantages (2 ideas). Consider **who** is affected: workers, employers, society.
