# Writing Task 2 — Advantages and disadvantages

*You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.*

> **In many countries, the proportion of older people is steadily increasing. What are the advantages and disadvantages of this trend?**

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

*Write at least 250 words.*

---

### How to approach it
- Think about the economy, healthcare, families and communities.
- No opinion is required, but a brief evaluative conclusion is fine.
