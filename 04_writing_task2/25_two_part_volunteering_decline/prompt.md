# Writing Task 2 — Two-part question

*You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.*

> **In some countries, fewer people are doing voluntary work than in the past. Why is this happening? How can more people be encouraged to volunteer?**

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

*Write at least 250 words.*

---

### How to approach it
- Answer the 'why' and the 'how' in separate paragraphs.
- Link solutions to reasons.
