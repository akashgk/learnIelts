# Writing Task 2 — Opinion (agree / disagree)

*You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.*

> **Some people believe that the money spent on space exploration would be better spent solving problems on Earth. To what extent do you agree or disagree?**

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

*Write at least 250 words.*

---

### How to approach it
- Avoid vague claims; give concrete examples of benefits (satellites, weather forecasting, technology).
- Address the opposing view fairly before rebutting it.
