# Model essay (Band 8–9)

The rapid development of artificial intelligence has led some to predict that human teachers will soon become obsolete. Although I accept that AI will transform how students learn, I disagree that it will make teachers unnecessary, because education involves far more than the transfer of information.

It is true that AI offers remarkable advantages as a learning tool. Intelligent tutoring systems can explain a concept in several different ways, provide instant feedback on exercises and adapt the level of difficulty to each learner's progress — a degree of personalisation that a single teacher with thirty pupils cannot match. Such tools are available at any time of day and at a relatively low cost, which could make high-quality instruction accessible to students in remote or under-resourced areas.

However, the idea that students could learn everything they need from computers overlooks the human dimensions of education. Firstly, most learners, particularly children, need motivation and encouragement to persevere with difficult material. A teacher who notices that a pupil is struggling, anxious or bored, and responds with patience and enthusiasm, provides something a machine cannot easily replicate. Secondly, schools are where young people learn to cooperate, resolve conflicts and develop moral judgement, and these social skills are acquired through interaction with real people. Finally, AI systems can produce inaccurate or biased information, so students need guidance from knowledgeable adults to evaluate what they are told critically.

For these reasons, I believe that the role of teachers will evolve rather than disappear. As AI takes over routine tasks such as marking and drilling, teachers will be able to devote more time to mentoring, discussion and supporting students' well-being.

In conclusion, while AI will become an increasingly powerful educational tool, it will complement rather than replace human teachers.

## Useful language
- **Concession → rebuttal:** *It is true that… However, the idea that… overlooks…*
- **Prediction:** *will evolve rather than disappear; will complement rather than replace* — the *X rather than Y* structure is excellent for nuanced positions.
- **Vocabulary:** *obsolete, personalisation, under-resourced, persevere, replicate, biased, critically, mentoring, well-being, routine tasks*.
