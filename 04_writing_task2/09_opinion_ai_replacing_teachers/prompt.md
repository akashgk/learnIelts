# Writing Task 2 — Opinion (agree / disagree)

*You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.*

> **Some people believe that in the future, artificial intelligence will make teachers unnecessary, as students will be able to learn everything they need from computers. To what extent do you agree or disagree?**

*Write at least 250 words.*

---

### How to approach this question type
- Technology questions invite extreme answers. A nuanced position ("AI will change the teacher's role, but not replace it") is usually more convincing.
- Distinguish between **information** (which AI can provide) and **education** (motivation, judgement, social development).
