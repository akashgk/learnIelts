# Writing Task 2 — Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?

*You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.*

> **Home DNA testing kits, which reveal information about a person's ancestry and health risks, are becoming increasingly popular. Do the advantages of this outweigh the disadvantages?**

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

*Write at least 250 words.*

---

### How to approach it
- Requires a clear verdict.
- Consider health, privacy, emotional impact and family relationships.
