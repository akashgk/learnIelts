# Writing Task 2 — Problem / solution (causes and solutions)

*You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.*

> **Traffic congestion is becoming a serious problem in many major cities. What are the main causes of this problem, and what measures could be taken to solve it?**

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

*Write at least 250 words.*

---

### How to approach this question type
- Answer **both** questions: causes AND solutions. One body paragraph each works well.
- Make solutions **match** the causes you identified — this shows logical coherence.
- Solutions should be specific (who does what?) rather than vague ("the government should do something").
