# Writing Task 2 — Problem / solution

*You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.*

> **Noise pollution is a growing problem in many cities. What are the causes of this problem? What measures could be taken to reduce it?**

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

*Write at least 250 words.*

---

### How to approach it
- Two questions: causes and measures.
- Specific solutions score higher than general ones.
