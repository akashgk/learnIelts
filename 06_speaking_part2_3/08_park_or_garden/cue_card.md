# Speaking Part 2 & 3 — A Park or Garden You Like

## Part 2 · Cue card

> *You will have to talk about the topic for one to two minutes. You have one minute to think about what you are going to say. You can make some notes to help you if you wish.*

**Describe a park or garden that you like to visit.**

You should say:
- where it is
- what it looks like
- what people do there
- and explain why you like to visit it

## Part 3 · Discussion questions

*The examiner will ask more abstract questions linked to the Part 2 topic (4–5 minutes).*

1. Why are parks important in cities?
2. Should city land be used for parks or for housing?
3. How do people's leisure activities in parks differ between generations?
