# Speaking Part 2 & 3 — A Person Who Influenced You

## Part 2 · Cue card

> *You will have to talk about the topic for one to two minutes. You have one minute to think about what you are going to say. You can make some notes to help you if you wish.*

**Describe a person who has had an important influence on your life.**

You should say:
- who this person is
- how long you have known them
- what qualities this person has
- and explain why they have influenced you so much

## Part 3 · Discussion questions

*The examiner will ask more abstract questions linked to the Part 2 topic (4–5 minutes).*

1. Who usually has more influence on children — parents or teachers?
2. Do you think celebrities are good role models for young people?
3. How has the role of teachers changed in recent years?
