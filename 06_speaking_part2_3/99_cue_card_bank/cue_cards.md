# Speaking Parts 2 & 3 — Cue card bank

More cue cards in the current IELTS style, each with linked Part 3 questions. Practise with a timer: **1 minute** of notes, **2 minutes** of speaking, then answer the Part 3 questions (answer → reason → example → contrast).

## 1. Describe a time you were late for something important.
You should say:
- what it was
- why you were late
- what happened
- and how you felt about it

**Part 3:**
- Why are some people often late?
- Is punctuality more important in some cultures than others?
- How has technology changed the way we manage time?

## 2. Describe a website or app that you find useful.
You should say:
- what it is
- how you found it
- what you use it for
- and explain why it is useful

**Part 3:**
- What are the most popular apps in your country?
- Do people rely too much on apps?
- How might apps change in the future?

## 3. Describe an old object that your family has kept for a long time.
You should say:
- what it is
- where it came from
- how long your family has had it
- and why it is important to your family

**Part 3:**
- Why do people keep old things?
- Do young people value old objects?
- Should old buildings be preserved?

## 4. Describe a time you received good news.
You should say:
- what the news was
- how you received it
- who you shared it with
- and how you felt

**Part 3:**
- How do people usually share good news nowadays?
- Is there too much bad news in the media?
- Do people prefer to hear news from friends or the media?

## 5. Describe a person you know who is good at their job.
You should say:
- who this person is
- what job they do
- how you know them
- and why you think they are good at it

**Part 3:**
- What makes someone successful at work?
- Is job satisfaction more important than salary?
- Should people stay in the same job for a long time?

## 6. Describe a city you would like to visit in the future.
You should say:
- which city it is
- how you learned about it
- what you would do there
- and why you want to visit

**Part 3:**
- Why do people like to visit big cities?
- What problems does tourism cause in cities?
- Will people travel more or less in the future?

## 7. Describe a sport or physical activity you enjoy.
You should say:
- what it is
- when you started
- how often you do it
- and why you enjoy it

**Part 3:**
- Why do some people not exercise?
- Should governments promote sport?
- Are professional athletes good role models?

## 8. Describe a time you had to make a difficult decision.
You should say:
- what the decision was
- when it happened
- what options you had
- and explain how you made the decision

**Part 3:**
- What decisions do young people find difficult?
- Should parents make decisions for their children?
- Do people make better decisions alone or in groups?

## 9. Describe a film or TV series that made you think.
You should say:
- what it was
- when you watched it
- what it was about
- and why it made you think

**Part 3:**
- Why are documentaries popular?
- Do films influence people's behaviour?
- Will cinemas disappear in the future?

## 10. Describe a time you learned something from a mistake.
You should say:
- what the mistake was
- when it happened
- what the result was
- and what you learned

**Part 3:**

- Should schools punish students for mistakes?
