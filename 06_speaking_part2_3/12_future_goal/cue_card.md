# Speaking Part 2 & 3 — A Goal for the Future

## Part 2 · Cue card

> *You will have to talk about the topic for one to two minutes. You have one minute to think about what you are going to say. You can make some notes to help you if you wish.*

**Describe a goal you would like to achieve in the future.**

You should say:
- what the goal is
- when you set this goal
- what you need to do to achieve it
- and explain why this goal is important to you

## Part 3 · Discussion questions

*The examiner will ask more abstract questions linked to the Part 2 topic (4–5 minutes).*

1. Is it important for young people to set goals?
2. What stops people from achieving their goals?
3. Do you think success is mainly due to hard work or luck?
