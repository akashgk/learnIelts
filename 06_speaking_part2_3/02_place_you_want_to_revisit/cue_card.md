# Speaking Part 2 & 3 — A Place You Want to Revisit

## Part 2 · Cue card

> *You will have to talk about the topic for one to two minutes. You have one minute to think about what you are going to say. You can make some notes to help you if you wish.*

**Describe a place you have visited that you would like to go back to.**

You should say:
- where it is
- when you went there
- what you did there
- and explain why you would like to go back

## Part 3 · Discussion questions

*The examiner will ask more abstract questions linked to the Part 2 topic (4–5 minutes).*

1. Why do some people prefer to visit the same place again and again?
2. What are the effects of tourism on natural areas?
3. Should the number of tourists visiting some places be limited?
