# Speaking Part 2 & 3 — Useful Advice You Received

## Part 2 · Cue card

> *You will have to talk about the topic for one to two minutes. You have one minute to think about what you are going to say. You can make some notes to help you if you wish.*

**Describe a piece of good advice you received.**

You should say:
- who gave you the advice
- what the advice was
- when you received it
- and explain why you think it was good advice

## Part 3 · Discussion questions

*The examiner will ask more abstract questions linked to the Part 2 topic (4–5 minutes).*

1. Who do young people usually ask for advice?
2. Is it a good idea to get advice from the internet?
3. Why do some people find it difficult to accept advice?
