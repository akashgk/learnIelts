# Speaking Part 2 & 3 — A Skill You Are Proud Of

## Part 2 · Cue card

> *You will have to talk about the topic for one to two minutes. You have one minute to think about what you are going to say. You can make some notes to help you if you wish.*

**Describe a skill that you learned and are proud of.**

You should say:
- what the skill is
- when and how you learned it
- how difficult it was
- and explain why you are proud of it

## Part 3 · Discussion questions

*The examiner will ask more abstract questions linked to the Part 2 topic (4–5 minutes).*

1. What skills do you think children should learn at school apart from academic subjects?
2. Is it easier to learn new skills as a child or as an adult?
3. Do you think technology has changed the way people learn skills?
