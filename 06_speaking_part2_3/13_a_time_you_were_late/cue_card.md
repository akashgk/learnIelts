# Speaking Part 2 & 3 — A Time You Were Late

## Part 2 · Cue card

> *You will have to talk about the topic for one to two minutes. You have one minute to think about what you are going to say. You can make some notes to help you if you wish.*

**Describe a time when you were late for something important.**

You should say:
- what you were late for
- why you were late
- what happened as a result
- and how you felt about it

## Part 3 · Discussion questions

*The examiner will ask more abstract questions linked to the Part 2 topic (4–5 minutes).*

1. Why are some people often late?
2. Is punctuality more important in some cultures than others?
3. How has technology changed the way we manage time?
