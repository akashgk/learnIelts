# Speaking Part 2 & 3 — A Memorable Journey

## Part 2 · Cue card

> *You will have to talk about the topic for one to two minutes. You have one minute to think about what you are going to say. You can make some notes to help you if you wish.*

**Describe a memorable journey you have made.**

You should say:
- where you went
- how you travelled
- who you were with
- and explain why it was memorable

## Part 3 · Discussion questions

*The examiner will ask more abstract questions linked to the Part 2 topic (4–5 minutes).*

1. Do you think people will travel more or less in the future?
2. What are the advantages of travelling by train rather than by plane?
3. Why do some people enjoy travelling alone?
