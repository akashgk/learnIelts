# Speaking Part 2 & 3 — Something That Made You Proud

## Part 2 · Cue card

> *You will have to talk about the topic for one to two minutes. You have one minute to think about what you are going to say. You can make some notes to help you if you wish.*

**Describe something you did that made you feel proud.**

You should say:
- what you did
- when you did it
- who was involved
- and explain why you felt proud

## Part 3 · Discussion questions

*The examiner will ask more abstract questions linked to the Part 2 topic (4–5 minutes).*

1. Should parents praise their children a lot?
2. What achievements are valued in your society?
3. Is it better to work alone or in a team to achieve goals?
