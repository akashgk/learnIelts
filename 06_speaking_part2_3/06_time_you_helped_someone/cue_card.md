# Speaking Part 2 & 3 — A Time You Helped Someone

## Part 2 · Cue card

> *You will have to talk about the topic for one to two minutes. You have one minute to think about what you are going to say. You can make some notes to help you if you wish.*

**Describe a time when you helped someone.**

You should say:
- who you helped
- when it happened
- how you helped them
- and explain how you felt about helping this person

## Part 3 · Discussion questions

*The examiner will ask more abstract questions linked to the Part 2 topic (4–5 minutes).*

1. Why do some people not help others?
2. Should schools teach children to help others?
3. Do you think people help each other less than in the past?
