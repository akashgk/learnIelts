# Speaking Part 2 & 3 — An Interesting Book

## Part 2 · Cue card

> *You will have to talk about the topic for one to two minutes. You have one minute to think about what you are going to say. You can make some notes to help you if you wish.*

**Describe a book you read that you found interesting.**

You should say:
- what the book is
- when you read it
- what it is about
- and explain why you found it interesting

## Part 3 · Discussion questions

*The examiner will ask more abstract questions linked to the Part 2 topic (4–5 minutes).*

1. Why do some people prefer reading books to watching films?
2. Do you think e-books will completely replace printed books?
3. How can parents encourage children to read more?
