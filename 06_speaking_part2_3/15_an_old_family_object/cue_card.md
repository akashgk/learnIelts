# Speaking Part 2 & 3 — An Old Family Object

## Part 2 · Cue card

> *You will have to talk about the topic for one to two minutes. You have one minute to think about what you are going to say. You can make some notes to help you if you wish.*

**Describe an old object that your family has kept for a long time.**

You should say:
- what it is
- where it came from
- how long your family has had it
- and explain why it is important to your family

## Part 3 · Discussion questions

*The examiner will ask more abstract questions linked to the Part 2 topic (4–5 minutes).*

1. Why do people keep old things?
2. Do young people value old objects?
3. Should old buildings be preserved?
