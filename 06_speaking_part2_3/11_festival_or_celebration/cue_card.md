# Speaking Part 2 & 3 — A Festival or Celebration

## Part 2 · Cue card

> *You will have to talk about the topic for one to two minutes. You have one minute to think about what you are going to say. You can make some notes to help you if you wish.*

**Describe a festival or celebration that is important in your country.**

You should say:
- what the festival is
- when it is celebrated
- what people do during it
- and explain why it is important

## Part 3 · Discussion questions

*The examiner will ask more abstract questions linked to the Part 2 topic (4–5 minutes).*

1. Why are traditional festivals important?
2. Do you think festivals have become too commercial?
3. How do young people's attitudes to festivals differ from older people's?
