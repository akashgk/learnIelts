# Speaking Part 2 & 3 — A Difficult Decision

## Part 2 · Cue card

> *You will have to talk about the topic for one to two minutes. You have one minute to think about what you are going to say. You can make some notes to help you if you wish.*

**Describe a time when you had to make a difficult decision.**

You should say:
- what the decision was
- when it happened
- what options you had
- and explain how you made the decision

## Part 3 · Discussion questions

*The examiner will ask more abstract questions linked to the Part 2 topic (4–5 minutes).*

1. What decisions do young people find difficult?
2. Should parents make decisions for their children?
3. Do people make better decisions alone or in groups?
