# Speaking Part 2 & 3 — A Piece of Technology You Use

## Part 2 · Cue card

> *You will have to talk about the topic for one to two minutes. You have one minute to think about what you are going to say. You can make some notes to help you if you wish.*

**Describe a piece of technology (not a phone) that you use often.**

You should say:
- what it is
- when you got it
- what you use it for
- and explain how it has changed your life

## Part 3 · Discussion questions

*The examiner will ask more abstract questions linked to the Part 2 topic (4–5 minutes).*

1. How has technology changed the way people work?
2. Do you think older people find it hard to keep up with technology?
3. Are there any disadvantages of relying too much on technology?
