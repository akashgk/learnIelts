# Speaking Part 2 & 3 — A Useful App or Website

## Part 2 · Cue card

> *You will have to talk about the topic for one to two minutes. You have one minute to think about what you are going to say. You can make some notes to help you if you wish.*

**Describe a website or app that you find useful.**

You should say:
- what it is
- how you found out about it
- what you use it for
- and explain why it is useful

## Part 3 · Discussion questions

*The examiner will ask more abstract questions linked to the Part 2 topic (4–5 minutes).*

1. What are the most popular apps in your country?
2. Do people rely too much on apps?
3. How might apps change in the future?
